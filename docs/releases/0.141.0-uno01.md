# Uno Launcher 0.141.0-uno01

Every Hyper Glass look can now be found, Hyper Glass Cards have real chromatic aberration and a grain that does not repeat, and an app's live activity steps off the island while that app is open.

## What's changed

### Hyper Glass icons: a menu that shows every look

In 0.140.0 the Icon Style menu had eleven entries and showed about eight before it scrolled, with no sign that it scrolled, so the app-colour and clear looks could not be found. Now:

- **Appearance › App Icons › Icon Style** has six entries: Original, Themed, Tinted, Dark, Auto, **Hyper Glass**.
- While Hyper Glass is chosen, a second row, **Hyper Glass Look**, has the six looks, named by picture and card: **App Colors on Black**, **App Colors on Clear**, **White on Clear**, White on Black, Black on White, White on Accent.
- Choosing Hyper Glass for the first time starts in App Colors on Black. What you had chosen is kept.

### Hyper Glass Cards (experimental): aberration, not a coloured rim

- **The split of colour is on what the card holds.** Words, icons and the grain have their red and blue read a little to either side of their green: nothing at the card's middle, 1.1 dp at its edge, as through a simple lens, and further while Home's pages move. In 0.140.0 the split was only in the blurred wallpaper, where it could not be seen, and a border drawn in red and in blue stood in for it.
- **The border is plain** again: one fine white line.
- **The grain does not repeat.** It was one small tile of noise, whose pattern showed across a card. It is now two tiles of different sizes, one turned, at the same strength.
- A folder's tile on Home is frosted glass again; it had become a small grain pane.

### Live activities step aside in their own app

With the island shown over other apps, an app's live activity leaves the island while that app is on screen, and comes back when you leave it: **directions no longer cover the map while Maps is open**, music steps aside in the player, a ride in its app. Each activity is judged by its own app, so with Maps open a ride's update still shows.

- This is **Island › Notifications › Step Aside in Its App**. It existed, off by default; it is now on unless you turn it off, and **is turned on once by this update** if it was off.
- It also now takes away every update of the app in front, not only the first, and the card and the quick actions follow.
- Needs the island over other apps and Uno's accessibility service.

## Privacy

For an activity to step aside, Uno's accessibility service is told the name of each app that comes to the front, and nothing else about it; only the latest name is kept, in memory, never stored or sent. That was true before only if you had turned the switch on; it is now true by default while the island is shown over other apps, and stops when Step Aside in Its App and Hide Over Chosen Apps are both off. `PRIVACY.md` says so.

## Notes: what this has and hasn't been tested on

Everything here was tested on an emulator (Android 16) with the test build (Uno Dev). **None of it has been run on a phone.**

**Stepping aside was not exercised on a device at all**: the emulator has no navigation running over an app. What decides it has unit tests (which updates go and which stay), and the code that reads the app in front is the code Hide Over Chosen Apps already used.

**Hyper Glass Cards:** seen on the feed, the App Library, a long-press menu and a settings menu, in dark and light, and held to a device test (what is on a card is split into colour; its edge is not). What the effect costs while pages move could not be measured: the emulator's frame times were noise with it on and off. Not seen: the search results.

**The grain** was looked at closely on the emulator, whose screen is scaled, so its size on a phone was not judged.

**The icon menus** were opened on the emulator and show every entry without scrolling.

1,540 unit tests pass and lint is clean.
