# Uno Launcher 0.16.14-uno01

A truer-black dynamic island with live activities, a selection lens on the page dots, and the Inter typeface.

## What's new

- **True-black island.** The pill's body is now pure OLED black, so the camera hole disappears into it. The glass rim and lens refraction you liked are kept as a thin outer ring.
- **Playback indicator.** While audio plays, a four-bar equalizer animates beside the camera hole. Expand the island for previous, play/pause and next. These send media keys, so they work with whatever is playing, but the island can't show track titles or artwork: that needs notification access, which Uno deliberately doesn't ask for. The controls stay for 90 seconds after playback stops so you can resume.
- **Island events.** The pill widens to show ringer changes (Silent, Vibrate, Ringer), airplane mode, Do Not Disturb and charging with its percentage, with a small spring pulse. Turning Do Not Disturb on no longer also flashes "Silent". Tapping the island gives a light haptic tick.
- **Selection lens.** A clear glass lens on the page dots follows the pager, magnifies the page or icon it is on, and lifts under your finger. Drag along the dots to scrub through pages. Dock icons get a brief press lens too.
- **Inter typeface** throughout, bundled in the app (SIL Open Font License, listed in the in-app notices). No font download, no network.
- **Fixes.** A tap on the island could land on its hidden panel's buttons instead of the island, and audio-system calls no longer run on the main thread, where they could stall the UI.

## Notes: what this release has and hasn't been tested on

This build has only run on emulators. Please try it on your Pixel and report anything off, especially:

- **Playback detection and the transport buttons** were not verified end to end: I couldn't get the emulator to play audio reliably, so I confirmed the equalizer and layout with a forced state and the logic with unit tests. If the equalizer doesn't appear while music plays, or the buttons don't control your player, that is a real bug to report.
- **Haptics** can't be felt on an emulator.
- **Ringer, airplane mode and Do Not Disturb** events were triggered on the emulator and appeared as designed.
- **Performance and battery** of the glass effects (the lens, the edge blur) are unmeasured on real hardware. If you notice slowdown, turn off **Liquid glass**.
- On a phone with a camera cutout the island wraps it; the pill's exact height depends on your cutout, and `adb shell dumpsys window displays | grep mDisplayCutout` gives the geometry if it looks off.
- This is a beta; report problems with your device model and Android version.

## Privacy

Uno has no account service, analytics, or automatic crash upload, and no new permissions in this release. See the [privacy notes](../PRIVACY.md).
