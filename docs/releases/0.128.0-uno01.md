# Uno Launcher 0.128.0-uno01

Search as on iOS 27, with the island as its bar; the open island rearranged round the camera; and faster glass.

## What's changed

- **Search is now the island itself**: a bar that is black round the camera and fades into liquid glass at its bottom edge, with results on a frosted card floating under it and Home left in view.
- **The open island, rearranged**: the date and battery beside the camera, one large time, and Search as a wide pill with Feed and Settings as round buttons.
- **Faster glass**: widgets and the iOS layout's dock share one blurred wallpaper, and frosted glass, the edge blur and the backgrounds behind folders and menus render at half size. On a Pixel 10a against 0.127.3, a page swipe's median frame went from 21.0 to 18.9 ms and a settings sheet's from 18.7 to 16.5 ms.
- Includes everything in 0.127.3-uno01: an island that lands exactly and grows its contents with it.

## Notes: what this has and hasn't been tested on

1,168 unit tests pass (1 skipped). On an emulator: the island's and search's device tests, the new glass against the live glass on a test pattern, and the open island's layout. On a Pixel 10a: frame timings against 0.127.3, the dock's glass, and the new search opening, closing and with long text. **Not seen:** the open island's layout around the Pixel's real camera, a full row of four suggestions, and a Home full of glass widgets. Search has no mic button, as Uno has no voice search.
