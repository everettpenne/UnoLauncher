# Uno Launcher 0.97.0-uno01

A live island in its settings, a regrouped island page, and wallpaper you can adjust and preview before applying.

## What's new

- The island page keeps a live copy of the island at the top. Changing a setting that affects how the island looks or behaves plays it there first, and chips under it show any situation again (idle, music, a Live Update, three activities, a notification, the camera in use, open).
- The island's settings are grouped: The island, How it looks and moves, What shows in the island, New notifications (moved from Notifications & privacy), Timer, and Where things show.
- Wallpaper: a chosen photo can be dragged into place, zoomed, and given brightness, blur, colour and warmth in a phone-shaped preview before Apply. An applied photo can be adjusted again from the original.
- Built-in backgrounds are previewed on Home with Apply and Cancel instead of changing at once.
