# Uno Launcher 0.137.0-uno01

The Style dial's Expressive level reaches further: search and the assistant, Settings, menus, sheets and folders, and the island's cards. Balanced, the default, is unchanged but for one loader.

## What's changed

Everything below is at **Expressive** (Settings › Appearance › Style). Calm is untouched and there are no new settings.

- **Three colours instead of one.** Expressive's tonal fills now draw on your accent and a second and third colour taken from the wallpaper, as Pixel's do. A wallpaper of one colour gives the accent's neighbours instead. The wallpaper's colours are used whatever Appearance › Accent is set to; the glass itself still takes no colour unless Accent is From Wallpaper.
- **Shapes that mean something.** The mark beside a row is a Material shape with a tonal fill, and each kind keeps its shape wherever it is met: a cookie for something the launcher does (a quick action in search, an action in a long-press menu), a soft burst for asking Qwen, a pill for going elsewhere (a web search), a flower for a person. Settings' sections take the shapes in turn, so no two neighbours are alike. A menu's Remove keeps its plain red glyph.
- **Names in the display face.** Settings' title, the titles of sheets and dialogs, a folder's name, and the headings of sections in search and in settings.
- **The conversation.** What you said is in a tonal bubble, and a chosen thumbs-up or thumbs-down sits on a tonal cookie.
- **The island's cards.** The stopwatch and the charge are in display numerals, and progress (the bar, the wave under music, the timer's ring) is thicker. The island's colours stay its theme's, and the pill is untouched.

At **Balanced**, one thing: while Qwen thinks, search shows Material's shape-morphing loader, the one My feed already shows at Balanced. Calm keeps the still mark.

## Notes: what this has and hasn't been tested on

**On an emulator, with the test build (Uno Dev), at Calm, Balanced and Expressive:** search before and after typing, with an action and a web search side by side; Settings' first page and a page of groups; the long-press menus of an app and a widget; the island's stopwatch card (with minutes in two digits) and timer card.

**Balanced against the build before this work,** screenshot against screenshot: Settings, search's results, the widget menu and the island's stopwatch card were the same.

1,484 unit tests pass (every new behaviour is held off at Calm and Balanced, and every colour of the palette is held readable on its fill), lint is clean, and the 15 assistant device tests pass.

**Not seen on any screen:** the loader while Qwen thinks, the tonal bubble and the thumb's cookie (the emulator has no Uno Lab); a folder's name and a dialog's title in the display face, which may wrap with a long name; a contact's flower; the charging card and the thicker wave under music; the dark theme; the dock's long-press menu; changing Style while a screen is open (every check restarted the launcher first). Nothing in this release was seen on a phone.
