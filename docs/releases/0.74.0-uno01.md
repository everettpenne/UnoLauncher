# Uno Launcher 0.74.0-uno01

Spotlight and the keyboard move as one, sliders glide, and a slow swipe up closes the island.

## What's new

- **Spotlight and the keyboard move as one.** The keyboard is requested on the very frame the island begins to open and hidden the frame the close begins (it used to appear a beat late and linger through the exit animation). The window no longer resizes for the keyboard — Home, the dock and the rail never budge — and the page dots and search button ride the keyboard's top edge frame by frame, both directions.
- **Sliders glide.** Settings sliders moved in stair-steps (each spring ran to a full stop before the next finger position was read). The thumb now tracks your finger 1:1 while you drag, with the liquid stretch still reading your speed, and springs — with your velocity — only on taps and programmatic changes.
- **A slow swipe up closes the island.** A gentle drag up from the open island or a peek now tracks your finger (same low-pass as the pull-down) and closes past 32 dp of travel or with a quick flick. Resting a finger on the bottom handle is still "hold it open".

## Notes: what this has and hasn't been tested on

Unit tests pass (new: swipe-close thresholds, slider velocity math), lint is clean, and the debug build runs on an Android 16 emulator. **Not run on a real phone** — the keyboard choreography and the tracked swipe-up are precisely the things to feel. See `docs/device-checklist.md`.
