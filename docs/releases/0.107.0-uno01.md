# Uno Launcher 0.107.0-uno01

A livelier music island, symmetric widths on a busy status bar, and iOS's corners.

## What's changed

- **Music**: the open island's music card has a wavy progress line, a filled play button in the album's colour, and playing bars at its corner; the flame of the album's colour under the pill is stronger and flickers more.
- **A busy status bar**: with Uno's alignment service on, the island picks the symmetric width that cuts the least of any icon, never over the clock or battery. The alignment page no longer says "Off" while the service is running.
- **Corners** follow the suite and iOS: settings cards are 26 dp, nested in a 42 dp sheet.
- **The island settings preview** matches the real island.

## Install
