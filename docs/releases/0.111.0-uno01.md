# Uno Launcher 0.111.0-uno01

A new middle level for the island, a Playful theme, and a Home that is closer to iOS.

## What's changed

- **Island cards**: long-press the pill for a card made for music, a call, a timer, a stopwatch, a Live Update (a download gets a wavy ring) or navigation. The island stays black over the camera; colour is only a glow that fades out before the hole.
- **Playful theme** (on by default, Island › Style › Playful or Calm): rolling digits, shape-morphing buttons, wavy progress, a light edge, a liquid battery, livelier capsules.
- **Home**: seven rows, a hold-menu on apps, iOS-style folders and Edit Home, a widget placement bar, Card and Glass widget styles, and the App Library (renamed from All apps) with a cheaper frosted glass.
- **Welcome flow**: five steps.

## Notes: what this has and hasn't been tested on

Run on a Pixel 10a (GrapheneOS): the timer, stopwatch and music cards (the music card with a real track), the cutout veil, the liquid battery, the seven-row migration on real data, the hold menus, folders and edit mode, and the frosted library. Unit tests and lint pass. **Not tried with real data:** the call, Live Update, download and navigation cards (only their layouts, in a test screen). The instrumented tests were not re-run for this release; on 0.110.0 20 of 261 failed on the phone for environment reasons.
