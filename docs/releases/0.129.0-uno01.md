# Uno Launcher 0.129.0-uno01

The open island ends in liquid glass, and Uno's widgets read as one family.

## What's changed

- **The open island ends in liquid glass**: on Home its bottom edge turns from black into the same clear glass as search's bar. It stays black round the camera and behind the clock and cards, and stays black while it opens and over other apps.
- **Music in the glass**: while a track plays, its cover's colour tints that glass.
- **The date sits opposite the time**, out of the band beside the camera, where the rounded corner curved in under it.
- **Uno widgets read as one family**: one size and colour for big numbers, big times with a small AM or PM, units on the number's baseline, and one caption style.
- Screen time's small widget no longer cuts off an app row, and Glance's alarm chip fits "Tomorrow".
- Includes everything in 0.128.2-uno01: the battery left to the status bar, and a running timer on the open island.
