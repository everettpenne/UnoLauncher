# Uno Launcher 0.17.1-uno01

A truer glass edge, a control-panel dimming fix, and a Color from wallpaper setting.

## What's new

- **Directional rim light.** Glass surfaces (widgets, dock, panels, sheets, the page-dots lens and the island) now have a specular highlight lit from the upper left, instead of an even outline, so the glass reads as having thickness.
- **Color from wallpaper** (Customize > Wallpaper & appearance, off by default). Takes one color from your wallpaper, photo or dunes, and uses it to tint the glass, sliders, switches and themed icons. It adapts to light and dark, and the text stays readable on any wallpaper.
- **Control panel dimming fix.** The dimming behind the panel now covers the whole screen. It used to stop at the status and navigation bars, leaving two strips at normal brightness that looked like the edges of a rectangle behind the panel.

## Notes: what this release has and hasn't been tested on

This build has only run on an emulator that renders in software, so it is slow there and it threw "not responding" dialogs in heavier flows, as 0.16.14 does; real-hardware performance is unmeasured. If glass feels slow, turn off **Liquid glass**.

Verified on the emulator: the rim light on widgets, dock, panel and island; the control panel's dimming in the status-bar area (the bottom strip on the emulator is a separate system taskbar window, so I could not check the navigation-bar strip there); Color from wallpaper on the dune wallpaper (sliders, switches, icons and sheets take its sand tone); and the contrast guarantees by unit test across ten test wallpapers in both themes.

Please check on your Pixel:

- **Both edges of the dimming behind the control panel**, top and bottom, with your gesture bar.
- **Color from wallpaper with a photo wallpaper**, in light and dark, and with Themed icons.
- Whether the rim light is too strong or too faint to your eye (one-line change to tune).
- Everything listed as unverified in the 0.17.0 notes (flashlight, track titles, real badges and peek, contact results, timer ringing, haptics).
