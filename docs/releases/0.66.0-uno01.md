# Uno Launcher 0.66.0-uno01

The island moves like liquid, and does more: buttons on notification cards, a camera and microphone row, Bluetooth connect alerts, and a size made for the Pixel 10a.

## What's new

- **Liquid motion.** It stretches as it opens and squashes as it closes, what is written on it blurs and grows in with the body, and a bubble leaving the pill stays joined to it by a neck that stretches thin and pinches off. Android's animations off turns all of it off.
- **Buttons on notification cards.** Reply, Mark as read, Archive and the other buttons the app put on the notification, under the card; a reply is typed in the island itself on Home. Under Notifications, "Action buttons on the card".
- **Camera and microphone row** on the open island: what is in use, for how long, a Review button, and (if you have already allowed usage access) the app that was in front when it started. Android does not tell any app which app is using the camera or microphone, so the island does not claim to know.
- **Bluetooth connect alerts** for watches, keyboards and cars, using Android's Nearby devices permission, asked for only when you switch the alert on. Headphones and speakers keep their own alert.
- **Sized for the Pixel 10a.** A thicker pill (27 dp or more, was a thin capsule), a panel 92 % of the screen wide (up to 372 dp) with 34 dp corners.

## Notes: what this has and hasn't been tested on

Unit tests pass, and the island's instrumented tests pass on an Android 16 emulator at 1080 x 2400 with a small round camera hole, including a reply typed in the island and sent through a notification's reply input, and a test that the island still composes only a handful of times per morph. **It has not been run on a real phone.** The Pixel 10a sizing uses the camera hole the phone reports, but its tuning is checked against a representative hole (about 19 dp, 18 dp from the top), not a measured one. The liquid motion was checked in single frames (the bubble's neck, the blur) rather than felt. The Bluetooth alert needs a real device. See `docs/device-checklist.md`.
