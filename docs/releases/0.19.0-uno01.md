# Uno Launcher 0.19.0-uno01

Liquid glass app icons, app shortcuts on long-press, your feeds on and off, and fixes.

## What's new

- **Liquid glass themed icons.** Themed icons are now translucent glass tiles with a soft glyph shadow, an upper-left sheen and a lit rim that matches the glass elsewhere. Apps without a themed icon get a silhouette of their own glyph. (Customize > Control panel & extras > App icons > Themed.)
- **App shortcuts.** Long-press an app and its own quick actions appear first in the options sheet: Camera's selfie, Chrome's incognito tab, Maps' Home and Work, and so on. This needs Uno to be your Home app.
- **Feeds on and off.** Every saved feed has a switch under Customize > News feed, so you can keep several links and show only some. Google Discover is removed as a default and as an option; the feed page is always the leading page.
- **Split screen fix.** The second app now waits until the first is actually in front, and cancels with a message instead of pairing with an unrelated app. I could not reproduce your wrong-first-app report on the emulator, so please tell me if it still happens.

## Notes: what this release has and hasn't been tested on

Tested only on an emulator that renders in software, so it is slow there and shows "not responding" dialogs in heavier flows; real-hardware performance is unmeasured.

Verified on the emulator: the glass icons (both monochrome-layer and silhouette cases), Maps' real shortcuts appearing and launching Maps' shortcut activity, the feed switches (an off feed's entries vanish from the page while a second feed's stay), and shortcut ordering and feed filtering by unit test.

Please check on your Pixel:

- **How the glass icons look on your wallpaper**, in light and dark, with Color from wallpaper on and off.
- **Shortcuts on apps you use**, including work-profile apps and Camera.
- **Split screen**, especially with a first app that was not already running.
- Everything still unverified from earlier releases: flashlight, track titles, real badges and peek, timer ringing, haptics, the tilt highlight on your sensor, and the control panel dimming at both edges.
