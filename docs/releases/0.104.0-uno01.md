# Uno Launcher 0.104.0-uno01

Settings reorganized like iOS, about half as many of them, and a wallpaper editor like iOS 26's.

## What's changed

- **Settings like iOS Settings**: grouped lists with short names and the detail in each group's footer. **Backup & about** is now **General**.
- **Fewer settings**: Glass is Clear, Frosted or Off; the accent is **Uno orange** or **from the wallpaper** (Material You is gone, and its users keep a wallpaper accent); one **System Alerts** switch for the island; a keyboard page of about 13 settings. The side dock stays, for foldables.
- **A full-screen wallpaper editor**: swipe for styles (Black & White, Duotone, Color Wash and more), pinch to frame, Blur and Adjust buttons, and a preview of your Home over the photo.
- **Concentric corners** throughout settings, the control panel, the App Library, dialogs and the open island.
- **A 13.5 mm resting island**, and **one glass language**: clear on Home, frosted on sheets and panels.
