# Uno Launcher 0.23.9-uno01

The music controls stay after you pause, the open island shows the album art and track above its controls, and a permission ledger and VPN and USB alerts bring the island closer to what a GrapheneOS phone is for.

## What's fixed

- **Controls disappearing after a pause.** The island worked out how long ago the music stopped using a clock that only ticks every few seconds, so right after a pause the answer came out negative and the controls were hidden while the island stayed open. A tap where the play button had been then started the music. They now stay for the usual 90 seconds.

## What's new

- **A music card.** The open island shows the artwork and the track name and artist above play, pause and skip, and the play button takes its colour from the cover. Tap the art or the title to open the player. The track and artwork need the "Track title and artwork in media" switch and notification access; without them the card shows the bars and "Playing" or "Paused".
- **Permission ledger.** Under Customize, Control panel & extras, a new section lists every permission the launcher uses, what it is for, what it can see, whether it is on, and a button to Android's settings to turn it off. A line at the top says which island you have right now: on Home only, above other apps but not tappable, or tappable.
- **Blocked service notice.** If the accessibility service is switched on but Android is not running it (Advanced Protection can do this), the ledger says so and the plain overlay is used instead.
- **VPN and USB alerts.** The island flashes when a VPN connects or drops, and when a USB data connection starts or ends. Plain charging is not announced. They need no extra permission and have a switch under Notifications.

## Download and install

Use **UnoLauncher-0.23.9-uno01-release.apk** from the release assets. Signed with the same key as previous Uno releases, so it installs directly over 0.23.8. It still cannot install over a jakesgoodapps Duo Launcher build: uninstall that build first.

## Notes: what this has and hasn't been tested on

On an emulator with a centred camera, a test page playing audio with a title, artist and artwork: the music card showed all three above the controls; pausing through the island kept the card with a play triangle for several seconds, then left the pill showing the artwork with dim, still bars. The wording of the ledger, the VPN and USB alerts, the USB data check and the controls timing are unit tested.

Not tested, so please check on your phone:

- **The permission ledger screen**: it was compiled and unit tested but I could not reach it on the slow emulator, so please open it and tell me if it reads well.
- **VPN and USB alerts** with a real VPN and a real USB cable (including a data connection and a charge-only one).
- **The music card with your players** (NewPipe): artwork, track name, the pause button and its tint.
