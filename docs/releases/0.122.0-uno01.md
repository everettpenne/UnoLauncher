# Uno Launcher 0.122.0-uno01

A keyboard that learns how you type, and an autocorrect that learns from you.

## What's changed

- **Typing habits**: keys fit where your taps really land, autocorrect gets more careful if you often undo it, each app keeps its layout, and the emoji you put after a word is offered first. See and reset it under Keyboard → Typing Habits.
- **Autocorrect**: a slip you fix twice by picking the right word is fixed for you after that; a correction you undo is never made again; days, months, languages and places get their capital; "i'm" becomes "I'm"; "sooo" is left alone; the second-best reading sits in the strip.
- **Suggestions**: long-press one to stop it being offered.
- **Privacy**: all of it stays on the phone and holds nothing you typed. PRIVACY.md is corrected (it still said there was no autocorrect).
