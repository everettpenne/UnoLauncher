# Uno Launcher 0.109.0-uno01

Smarter drop-downs, a quieter full screen, and shorter words on a busy status bar.

## What's changed

- **Smarter drop-downs**: People Only lets just messages from people drop the island down; a flooding chat is quieted for a couple of minutes; an app whose drop-downs you never open stops dropping down, listed under Quieted Automatically to bring back with a tap.
- **Full screen**: games and video keep the island away, except for a call or a ringing timer.
- **A busy status bar**: the island says it shorter (12m, 84%) when the full words would make it cut an icon.

## Notes: what this has and hasn't been tested on

Checked on a Pixel 10a (GrapheneOS) with a debug copy: on a busy status bar a Live Update's "12 min" became "12m" and the pill narrowed. The drop-down rules and the full-screen rule are unit-tested; neither was seen with real notifications, a call or a game on the phone.
