# Uno Launcher 0.106.0-uno01

A height slider for the island, and live activities that sit balanced on the pill.

## What's changed

- **Island › Height**: from the camera's own height (the default) to a little taller. It is always a true pill, centred on the camera, 16 mm wide.
- **Balanced live activities**: the call, timer, music or Live Update icon is smaller and sits in the pill's left end, with its time or detail mirrored at the right end. Music without artwork shows a note.
- **A more compact pill** while something is live.
- **Always symmetric**: the island no longer grows lopsided to fit a guessed status bar, which over apps could cut an icon in half.
