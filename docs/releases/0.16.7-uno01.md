# Uno Launcher 0.16.7-uno01

Liquid controls and iOS accents: the sliders and switches in customization are now glass, and the lens has three independent knobs.

## What's new

- **Liquid sliders and switches.** When liquid glass is on, customization sliders are iOS-style orange tracks with a refracting glass thumb (tap or drag anywhere on the track), and switches are green iOS toggles with a glass thumb that slides and glows. Controls inside panels sample the panel they sit on, so the glass is real, not decorative. With liquid glass off, the stock Material controls return.
- **Three lens knobs.** The single refraction slider is replaced by **Refraction height** (how wide the glass rim the lens bends), **Refraction amount** (how far the view behind is displaced), and **Chromatic aberration** (the color fringe at the edges), each 0–100%, persisted separately. Your previous refraction setting migrates automatically.
- **iOS oranges and greens.** Switches use Apple's green; slider tracks, thumbs' value labels, and highlights use Apple's orange.

## Notes

- Chromatic aberration is a lens property with an on/off in the shader; the slider engages it above about 5% and its position sets nothing else. High aberration plus high displacement is the most expensive combination on the GPU.
- This release is validated on emulators; treat it as a beta and report problems with your device model and Android version.

## Privacy

Uno has no account service, analytics, or automatic crash upload. The internet permission exists only for fetching https feed addresses you add. See the [privacy notes](../PRIVACY.md).
