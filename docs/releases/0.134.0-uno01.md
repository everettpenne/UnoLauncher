# Uno Launcher 0.134.0-uno01

Conversations with Qwen you can carry on, more it can do, and a bolder look: a headline on the island you choose, Pixel-style type and shapes on My feed and the App Library, and settings laid out with menus.

## What's changed

### Talking to Qwen

- **Conversations.** Pull down under an answer in search to open the conversation, ask the next thing, and read it back later. A follow-up asked while the last answer is still being written is answered too, and the unfinished answer is kept. Conversations stay on the phone, never in a backup; delete one in the conversation view, or all of them in Extras › Search.
- **Follow-ups to a task.** "Undo that", "again", "make it 15" and "and also…" act on what Qwen just did, at once and without the model where they can.
- **More it can do:** play, pause and skip what's playing; media volume; cancel a timer; add a line to the Checklist widget; directions to a place (handed to your maps app).
- **Times as you say them.** "An hour and a half", "tomorrow 3pm" and the like are read by the launcher, not the model, so they come out right.
- **The assistant's light.** A band of colour round the island's pill and search's field shows Qwen thinking, writing, waiting on you, or done.

### Look

- **The island's headline is yours.** The big line on the open island can show the time, a greeting of your own ("hello" to start), the day or the date, in a font you pick: Classic, System, Script, Chunky, Tall, Editorial, Marker, or Bagel (Bagel Fat One, bundled). Its colour can follow the text, the highlight, or sweep between two of the theme's colours. Two new island themes use them: Pop and Hello. Island › Style.
- **The small and wide island is solid black to its edge.** The glass rim and edge light now only come in as it opens.
- **My feed and the App Library, Pixel style.** Big, heavy page titles; the count of new stories as a numeral in Material's 9-sided cookie; the newest story on a large coloured card; each feed's letter in its own colour; the Library's letters and categories in coloured badges. Refreshing the feed shows Material's shape-morphing loader. The shapes are Google's own Material 3 Expressive outlines.
- **Neutral glass.** Glass no longer takes on the wallpaper's colour unless Accent is From Wallpaper. Folder tiles use the same glass as the dock, and My feed and the App Library are the same panel.

### Back gesture

- **Back follows your finger.** Swiping back eases search toward the island's pill and lets an open folder give, then closes on release or springs back if you change your mind (Android 14 and later).

### Settings

- **Menus for choices.** A setting with three or more choices is one row showing its value, opening a menu; two choices are a pair of buttons; on and off is a switch. The explanations sit under each group.
- **Font: Inter or System.** Appearance › Font sets Uno's text in Inter, as before, or in your phone's own font.
- **From Android's Settings.** App info › Uno Launcher › Additional settings in the app opens Uno's settings.

## Notes: what this has and hasn't been tested on

**On a Pixel 10a, with the test build (Uno Dev):**
- A follow-up during a streaming answer ("who painted the starry night", then "where did he live"), and a timer changed by "make it 10 minutes", with Uno Lab.
- The island headline in the settings preview in Script, Chunky and Bagel, with a 24-character greeting; the settings menus; the link from Android's App info; the system font; My feed and the App Library on a dark wallpaper; the feed's refresh loader, in the minified build.
- On an emulator: the real open island with the Script greeting writing in, and Bagel.

**In unit tests only:** the new tools (media, volume, timer cancel, checklist, directions), spoken times, and most follow-ups to a task.

**Not seen on a phone:** the folder tile's new glass (no folder on the test Home), the island's headline on your own Home (the test build's island is covered by the installed one), and My feed and the App Library on a bright wallpaper.
