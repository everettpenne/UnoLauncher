# Uno Launcher 0.73.0-uno01

A peek that doesn't fight the status bar, an extra-wide island option, sliders that slide again, and a keyboard that knows its contractions.

## What's new

- **The peek no longer fights the status bar.** Each gesture now has one owner: a pull that starts below the status-bar strip is the island's (peek, shade stays closed); a pull that starts inside the strip is the system's. With "Island everywhere" and the shade accessibility service on, the island floats above the status bar and the whole pill is draggable — that's the way to grab it anywhere.
- **Extra wide island.** "Permanent wide island" is now Off / Wide / Extra wide: Extra wide makes the wide pill half again as wide, clamped so it always keeps the screen's margins and clears the dock. Existing settings carry over.
- **A steadier finger.** Swiping the island closed tracks through a 40 ms low-pass filter — the bottom edge follows your finger without the twitch, and the settle keeps your filtered speed. Animations off: still 1:1.
- **Sliders slide again.** A settings slider's thumb froze while its percentage updated (its spring was cancelled every drag event); it now retargets without cancelling.
- **A finished navigation leaves the island.** A Live Update whose app dies without cancelling its notification no longer lingers; a once-a-minute sweep drops what Android says is gone.
- **Contractions.** 73 bare forms fix to their apostrophized word ("dont"→"don't", "oclock"→"o'clock"), real words are only ever offered, never stolen ("were" → "we're" in the lane), a correct "doesn't" is left alone, "ID" stays "ID", and a flung apostrophe completes ("don'"→"don't"). The keyboard still never learns or stores a word.

## Notes: what this has and hasn't been tested on

Unit tests pass (new suites for the wide-mode geometry and migration, the low-pass filter, the slider math, the live-update sweep, and eleven contraction tests; the false-change rate holds at 0.022%), and lint is clean.
