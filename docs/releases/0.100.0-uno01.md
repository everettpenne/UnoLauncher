# Uno Launcher 0.100.0-uno01

Widgets that look like iOS's, and an island and status bar that fit together on the iOS layout.

## What's changed

- **iOS-style widgets.** Each Uno widget's name is in its own colour, big figures are semibold, and Clock, Date and Battery are redrawn after the iPhone's.
- **On the iOS layout, the open island covers the status bar**, as on an iPhone: the clock and icons step aside while the island is open, peeking, being pulled, flashing an event or growing into search.
- **On the iOS layout, the idle island is a ring round the camera**, so it never covers a status bar icon; it widens when it has something to show (Extra wide still keeps it wide).
- **Status bar icons follow the wallpaper**: dark over a light top strip, light over a dark one.
