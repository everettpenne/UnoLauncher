# Uno Launcher 0.16.5-uno01

The glass is now real: pages slide beneath the dock and rail, every panel refracts Home, and the lens slider goes to 200%.

## What's new

- **Fixed backdrop sampling.** The wallpaper and the pager now record into separate layers that glass surfaces combine in order. Before this, the pager's recording overwrote the wallpaper's, so the dock and rail were sampling empty pixels and the lens had nothing to bend — only the tint and rim showed. The dock, rail, and page controls now refract the actual page content beneath them.
- **Home runs under the glass, iOS-style.** The pager extends beneath the dock and status rail, so page content slides under them during swipes and the lens has real edges to bend. Discover and All apps stay clipped to the visible page area; the resting layout is unchanged.
- **Glass everywhere it belongs.** The feed, Discover recovery, All apps panel, and widget gallery are now liquid surfaces over Home; the customization, first-run, app-action, and empty-space sheets are inset glass cards with a drag handle; the page-dot capsule and the search/back circle controls are glass too. Each surface keeps a flat fallback when liquid glass is off.
- **Refraction slider up to 200%.** The lens curve is stronger across the board (refraction height 14–40dp, displacement 32–120dp, depth-weighted), and chromatic aberration engages from 50%. The dock and rail are now more transparent — light tint, light blur — so the refraction carries the look instead of the frost.

## Notes

- High refraction costs more GPU time per surface; the slider is the tradeoff dial and the Liquid glass switch remains the off ramp.
- This release is validated on emulators; treat it as a beta and report problems with your device model and Android version.

## Privacy

Uno has no account service, analytics, or automatic crash upload. The internet permission exists only for fetching feed addresses you add. See the [privacy notes](../PRIVACY.md).
