# Uno Launcher 0.16.9-uno01

The dynamic island now wraps the camera cutout instead of sitting beside it.

## What changed

- **The island wraps the front-camera hole.** Collapsed, it's a capsule centered on the hole and symmetric around it — the time on one side, battery or the charging bolt on the other, so nothing hides behind the camera. It's dark enough to read as one piece with the hole. Expanded, the panel stays centered on the cutout and hangs below it, with its content starting underneath the hole.
- **Any hole position works.** A hole near the top edge shrinks the island to fit; a hole in a corner or flush against an edge — like the Pixel Fold's inner camera — is still fully wrapped. With no cutout at all, it's a plain capsule below the status bar.
- **Fixed the coordinate bugs that misplaced it.** The old layout mixed three coordinate systems (centering inside a safe-area-padded parent and then adding the cutout's absolute offset, treating pixels as dp, and reading insets once too early). Layout is now a small pure function, `IslandGeometry`, that works entirely in window pixels and re-reads the live window insets on every layout, so rotation and folding re-anchor the island.
- 8 new unit tests cover the geometry (170 total).

## Not yet confirmed on real hardware

- **Pixel 10a:** the geometry assumes a single centered punch-hole; that case is covered by unit tests, but the emulator can't produce a centered punch-hole, so it was exercised with an off-center edge-flush hole and a centered wide notch.
- **Tapping:** taps on the part of the island beside the hole expand it; taps directly on the hole itself did nothing on the emulator and are expected to behave the same on a real phone. The spring takes about a second to settle.
- **Status bar overlap:** the expanded panel overlaps the status bar on the emulator; worth a look on-device.

## Privacy

Uno has no account service, analytics, or automatic crash upload. The island reads only what the launcher already knows: the time, battery state, your feed cache, the app you launch, and the cutout geometry of Uno's own window. See the [privacy notes](../PRIVACY.md).
