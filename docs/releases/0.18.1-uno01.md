# Uno Launcher 0.18.1-uno01

A stronger, easier-to-see tilt-following highlight, with a strength slider and a sensor readout.

## What's new

- **Tilt strength slider** (Customize > Wallpaper & appearance, under Tilt-following highlight). The effect in 0.18.0 was too gentle to see on a real phone. Strength now makes the light swing further for each degree you tilt and sharpens the rim so the lit edge stays bright while the opposite edges fade, which is what makes the movement obvious. The default is 60%; 100% is deliberately dramatic.
- **A live readout** under the slider tells you whether the motion sensor is reaching Uno ("Motion sensor working. Light angle 43° (45° when upright)"). Tilt the phone with the settings open and the angle should change. If it says there are no readings yet, and you use GrapheneOS, allow Sensors for Uno Launcher in its app settings.
- Smoother, more responsive tracking.

## Notes: what this release has and hasn't been tested on

Verified on an emulator that renders in software: with virtual accelerometer readings, a 20° tilt changed which glass edges were lit far more at 100% than at 0%, and the settings readout showed the live angle. I could not test with real hand motion or real phone sensors, and the emulator's physics cannot tell you how it feels.

Please check on your Pixel:

- **Open the settings page, turn tilt on, and tilt the phone.** The readout's angle should move. If it stays at 45° or says there are no readings, the sensor is not reaching the launcher (check GrapheneOS's Sensors permission for Uno), and I want to know.
- **Whether 60% is the right default** and whether 100% feels too much.
- Battery and smoothness with tilt on (the glass redraws when the light moves).
- Everything still unverified from earlier releases: split screen with your apps, flashlight, track titles, real badges and peek, timer ringing, haptics, and both edges of the dimming behind the control panel.
