# Uno Launcher 0.105.0-uno01

The island fits the Pixel 10a's camera, with quick actions, battery moments and a Lock Screen clock.

## What's changed

- **The resting island is as tall as the camera on the Pixel 10a.** 0.104.2's fix only applied to camera holes up to 28 dp across, and the 10a's is a little larger, so its pill was still padded above and below. It now recognises a punch hole by its shape.
- **Long-press the island** for the current activity's own buttons: +1 min or cancel a timer, pause or reset the stopwatch, skip or pause music, end a call.
- **Battery moments:** charging, fully charged, and low battery at 20% and 10%, over apps too.
- **Hide the island over apps you choose** (off by default). It needs Uno's accessibility service, which is then told only which app is in front.
- **Focus can follow Do Not Disturb.**
- **Lock Screen clock widget:** the date over a large time on the wallpaper, in five typefaces.
- **App Library categories**, worked out on the phone from each app's own category.
- **Work and Private Space notifications** say which profile they are from and badge the right app.
- **GrapheneOS's Sensors permission** is shown in the permission ledger.
- A transfer to a new phone no longer copies Uno's data.
- The island's system events keep running while a sheet is open or a full-screen app hides the island.

## Notes: what this has and hasn't been tested on

Unit tests and lint pass. On the emulator: the charging and low-battery flashes, Focus following Do Not Disturb, App Library categories, and the Lock Screen clock in the widget picker. The pill's height was checked against the geometry and its tests; the emulator has no 10a-like camera. **Not run on a real phone**: quick actions, hiding over apps, work-profile notifications and the Sensors row in particular.
