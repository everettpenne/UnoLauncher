# Uno Launcher 0.76.0-uno01

Spotlight is the island's search, through and through.

## What's new

- **One corner.** The search panel now uses the island's own 34 dp corner (a single constant), so it reads as the pill grown larger, not a separate sheet.
- **A touch shorter.** The panel sits 24 dp lower, with its results still scrolling.
- **Swipe up to close.** A swipe up from the bottom of the open Spotlight follows your finger and closes it like a peek — release past 32 dp or with a quick flick. Tap-outside still closes.
- **The island defers.** While Spotlight is open, tapping the camera cutout does nothing (no island underneath), and an incoming notification — including a turn-by-turn cue — shows only its dot instead of expanding the island. Close Spotlight and the island takes over again.
