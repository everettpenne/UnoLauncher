# Uno Launcher 0.16.8-uno01

The liquid-glass dynamic island: a capsule anchored to your camera cutout that lives, breathes, and expands — launcher-local, never over other apps.

## What's new

- **Dynamic island on Home.** A liquid-glass capsule sits at the camera cutout (or top-center without one). Collapsed it shows the time and a green charging bolt; **tap it** and it springs open into a live panel with time and date, battery, the top headline from your feed, and three quick actions — Search, Feed, Customize. It tucks itself back in after five seconds.
- **Event flashes.** Launching an app flashes its icon and name through the island; plugging in flashes **Charging**.
- **A Dynamic island switch** under **Customize launcher → Wallpaper & appearance**, next to the liquid glass settings. The island refracts Home through the same lens knobs as the rest of the glass.

## Design notes

- The island is intentionally launcher-local: it draws on Home only and does not mirror notifications or overlay other apps, so it needs no accessibility or notification permissions.
- Media tiles and notification mirroring are the natural next step and would require an optional notification-listener permission; they're not in this release.

## Notes

- Cutout geometry comes from the launcher's own window insets and degrades gracefully to top-center on devices without one.
- This release is validated on emulators; treat it as a beta and report problems with your device model and Android version.

## Privacy

Uno has no account service, analytics, or automatic crash upload. The island reads only what the launcher already knows: the time, battery state, your feed cache, and the app you launch. See the [privacy notes](../PRIVACY.md).
