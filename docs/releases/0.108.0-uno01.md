# Uno Launcher 0.108.0-uno01

A smarter island: it fits what it shows and brings forward what is about to matter.

## What's changed

- **The island fits what it shows.** A short time makes a small pill and a long status a wider one, always symmetric about the camera. A counting clock never makes it twitch, and event flashes are sized to their words.
- **What matters comes forward**: a timer in its last minute, a turn close ahead, a ride or delivery that is arriving, then back to what you chose.
- **Step Aside in Its App** (Island settings, off by default): music leaves the island while you are in the player, as on iPhone.

## Notes: what this has and hasn't been tested on

Checked on a Pixel 10a (GrapheneOS) with a debug copy: call, timer, stopwatch, music and Live Update pills and a charging flash, each sized to its content and centred on the camera. The urgency rules and Step Aside are unit-tested; neither was seen with a real timer, ride or player on the phone.
