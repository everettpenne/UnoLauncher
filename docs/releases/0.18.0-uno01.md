# Uno Launcher 0.18.0-uno01

Split screen, a tilt-following glass highlight, and search that answers.

## What's new

- **Split screen.** Long-press an app, choose **Open in split screen**, then pick the second app. On a phone they open stacked top and bottom; Android decides the arrangement for the screen. No accessibility service is needed. Work-profile apps can be the first app but not the second yet.
- **Tilt-following highlight** (Customize > Wallpaper & appearance, with Liquid glass; off by default). The glass edge light stays put in the room as you tilt the phone, so the shine slides across the glass. It reads the motion sensor only while Home is on screen. If you use GrapheneOS and have turned Sensors off for Uno, the light just stays where it is.
- **Search that answers.** Type `12*(3+4)`, `15% of 80`, `5 km in mi` or `212 f to c` into All apps search and the answer appears above the apps, with a tap to copy. Everything is computed on your device, and ordinary app names (`7-zip`, `2048`) still search apps.

## Notes: what this release has and hasn't been tested on

This build has only run on an emulator that renders in software, so it is slow there and has shown "not responding" dialogs in heavier flows; real-hardware performance is unmeasured.

Verified on the emulator: split screen end to end through the real menu (Gmail on top and Chrome below, both in multi-window mode, with accessibility off); the tilt highlight responding to virtual accelerometer readings (the lit edges swap between top/bottom and left/right as the phone is "tilted"); the calculator answer on screen and the conversions by unit test; and contact search, which is now visible working (the earlier release could not show it on screen).

Please check on your Pixel:

- **Split screen with your own apps.** It depends on Android's launch-beside behavior, which differs between versions; if the second app opens full screen instead, tell me your Android version. On a foldable's inner screen Android may place the apps side by side instead of stacked.
- **The tilt highlight's feel**: is it smooth, is the direction right, does it cost noticeable battery or add jank (it redraws every glass surface when the light moves). Turn it off in Customize if so.
- **Both edges of the dimming behind the control panel** (from 0.17.1), and everything listed as unverified in the 0.17.0 notes (flashlight, track titles, real badges and peek, timer ringing, haptics).
