# Uno Launcher 0.128.2-uno01

A calmer open island, with the battery left to the status bar.

## What's changed

- **The battery is off the open island**: the level, its icon and the "Charging" label are left to the status bar. The time to full stays.
- **A running timer or stopwatch shows its time** opposite the clock.
- **Nothing is clipped at the edge any more**: music covers, a held notification's text and Live Update cards were being cut by a rounded corner.
- **A calmer panel**: the date clears the rounded corner, labels go when the card below already says the same, and the feed headline is quieter, opens the feed when tapped, and takes one line when the panel has other things to show.
- Includes everything in 0.128.1-uno01: search as the island's own bar, a results card that follows what you type, and faster glass.
