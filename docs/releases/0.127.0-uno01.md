# Uno Launcher 0.127.0-uno01

Smoother paging, My feed in place of Google Discover, and an island that knows when to step aside.

## What's changed

- **Google Discover removed**: My feed, Uno's own RSS page, is always the page before the first Home page. No Google window is embedded any more.
- **Smoother paging**: on a Pixel 10a a page swipe's median frame went from about 35 ms to about 20 ms, and janky frames from 20 % to 6 %.
- **The island steps aside** for search, settings and the control panel, and comes back after; a ringing timer still gets through. Notifications that arrive meanwhile are kept as the recent card.
- **Search comes out of the island**: the field widens out of the pill and the rows follow it down.
- **Glass closer to iOS 26**: a gentler lens with a faint chromatic edge, and frosted glass that blurs more and tints less.
- **Home lines up** with My feed and the App Library, and returns straight to Home after you leave from either of them.

## Notes: what this has and hasn't been tested on

1,155 unit tests pass. On a Pixel 10a: page-swipe frame timings before and after, and My feed as the leading page. On an emulator: the island stepping aside for search, Home's alignment with My feed and the App Library, and returning straight to Home. **Not seen:** the Spotlight cascade and the island's alert behaviour on a phone; the frosted glass's lighter tint over a busy wallpaper.
