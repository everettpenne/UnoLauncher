# Uno Launcher 0.128.1-uno01

Search's results card now follows what you type, smoothly.

## What's changed

- **The results card resizes fluidly**: it animates to the height its results need instead of jumping with each letter, and a letter typed while it is moving carries on from its current speed.
- **Results fade and slide**: rows fade in and out and move to their new places as the results change.
- Includes everything in 0.128.0-uno01: search as the island's own bar, the rearranged open island, and faster glass.
