# Uno Launcher 0.71.0-uno01

A restyled news feed, a Spotlight that forgives typos, and grabs that land mid-flight.

## What's new

- **The news feed, restyled.** Story cards are ink-tinted glass instead of grey Material slabs: headline 17 sp semibold, summary 15 sp, source and time muted so they no longer compete with the headline. Unread stories get a small accent dot; read stories dim their text, not the whole card. The "new" count is a pill, per-feed errors show in a tinted callout with a glass retry, the feed search field and empty-page buttons are glass, and feed settings rows have separators.
- **Spotlight learns to spell.** "gmial" finds Gmail and "calndar" finds Calendar (exact and prefix matches still win). "wifi settings", "bluetooth settings" and "screen brightness" resolve to their settings pages, "countdown 5" starts the timer, and Enter now picks the top app, then your typed action, then the first contact, then a web search. The action row sits under an "Action" header, an empty Spotlight teaches what to type, your typed letters are bolded in app names, and the All apps list rubber-bands at its ends.
- **Grabs that land mid-flight.** Catching the island while it collapses re-opens it from the height you caught it at (it used to snap shut), and re-grabbing a list's rubber band mid-spring continues the curve from your finger instead of snapping to the edge.

## Notes: what this has and hasn't been tested on

Unit tests pass (including new suites for typo matching, Enter routing, the feed's byline formatting, and the mid-flight grab fixes), and lint is clean. **It has not been run on a real phone**; the mid-flight grabs especially are finger-feel changes. See `docs/device-checklist.md`.
