# Uno Launcher 0.26.0-uno01

A more iOS-like Home: no tap shadows, drag an app onto another to make a folder, swipe up for settings, and settings that are sorted into sensible pages.

## What's new

- **No shadow when you tap.** The grey ripple and the white glow over pressed icons are gone; icons just spring in slightly, faster than before.
- **Drag onto an app to make a folder.** Hold an app over the middle of another and drop; drop nearer the edge and it simply moves there.
- **Swipe up anywhere on Home** to open the launcher settings.
- **Settings reorganised** like iOS Settings: Look, Home, Island & keyboard, Privacy and More, with the old catch-all "Control panel & extras" split into Gestures & feel, Search, Control panel & Focus, Dynamic island, Keyboard, App icons and Notifications & privacy.

## Download and install

Use **UnoLauncher-0.26.0-uno01-release.apk** from the release assets. Signed with the same key as previous Uno releases, so it installs directly over 0.25.0. It still cannot install over a jakesgoodapps Duo Launcher build: uninstall that build first.

## Notes: what this has and hasn't been tested on

Swipe-up opening the new settings overview was seen on an emulator; unit tests cover the folder-drop rule. The emulator was too slow to judge anything about feel, so please check on a phone: dragging apps onto each other (middle versus edge), tapping icons, folders, swiping up (including starting on a widget), and each settings page.
