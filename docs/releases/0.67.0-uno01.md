# Uno Launcher 0.67.0-uno01

Peek at the island by pulling it down, hold it open, find the notification you just missed, read navigation while driving, and a smarter keyboard.

## What's new

- **Peek.** Pull the small island down to open it part-way, and let go to hold it as a peek: the time and the most relevant row under it. Pull on to open it fully, swipe up to close it. It tucks away by itself after five seconds.
- **Hold it open.** Rest a finger on the bottom of the open island and it stays open as long as you hold it.
- **A recent notification stays one tap away.** For 30 seconds after one arrives, a dot on the pill says so and the open island (or a peek) shows it, with its buttons, under the time.
- **Navigation you can read while driving.** A 72 dp arrow, the distance at 34 sp and the street at 20 sp, on the open island, on the turn card and larger on the pill. Tested with Organic Maps' notification layout; other apps' may differ.
- **Smarter autocorrect.** It reads the sentence, catches slips of a neighbouring key, fixes habitual misspellings and more contractions, and leaves informal words and names alone. It still never learns or stores anything you type.
- **A new logo**, in `docs/images/` (`uno-logo-*.png`).

## Notes: what this has and hasn't been tested on

Unit tests pass, and the island's instrumented tests pass on an Android 16 emulator at 1080 x 2400 with a small round camera hole, including pulling the island to a peek, holding the open island's bottom past the time it would close, a notification held for and expiring after 30 seconds, and a large navigation arrow. **It has not been run on a real phone.** The pull in particular: Android may take a downward drag that starts at the very top of the screen for the notification shade; the island treats a cancelled drag as a release where the finger was, so it should still peek, but this is the first thing to try on the Pixel. See `docs/device-checklist.md`.
