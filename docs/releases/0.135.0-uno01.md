# Uno Launcher 0.135.0-uno01

One dial from iOS to Pixel: a Style setting that decides how far Uno's type, shapes, colour and motion lean toward Pixel, while glass stays the material throughout. And widgets in Material's shapes, in glass that bends along them.

## What's changed

### Style: Calm, Balanced or Expressive

Appearance › Style is one choice in place of many switches.

- **Calm** is the plain iOS look: Inter titles, a plain "19 new" capsule on My feed, capsule controls, rounded-square widgets.
- **Balanced** is the default, and is how 0.134.0 looked, with two additions: things that are on or selected take the accent colour (the control panel's torch tile, the chosen ringer mode), and a control's corners tighten a little while it is pressed.
- **Expressive** goes furthest: the big numbers in Uno's widgets are set in a heavy display face; a selected chip rests squarer than its neighbours; control panel tiles are capsules when off and tight rounded squares when on; the volume and brightness sliders have a thick bar of colour for a track; presses spring back with more bounce.

### Widgets in Material's shapes

- **Shape.** Long-press one of Uno's own square widgets, choose Edit Widget, then Shape: Cookie or Sunny (with Style at Balanced or Expressive). The widget keeps its own layout, drawn a little smaller to sit inside the shape. Not for stacks or for apps' widgets, which draw their own backgrounds.
- **Glass that follows the shape.** A shaped widget set to Glass is liquid glass in that outline, and bends the wallpaper along its lobes as the dock and the rounded widgets bend it along theirs. My feed's count of new stories is a glass cookie too, wherever glass is on.

### Smaller things

- The Style dial, the text font, the island's headline and each widget's shape are now part of a settings backup. The font and headline you chose in 0.134.0 are kept.

## Notes: what this has and hasn't been tested on

**On a Pixel 10a, with the test build (Uno Dev):**
- Home and My feed at Calm, Balanced and Expressive; the display numerals in the clock and date widgets.
- The clock widget as a glass cookie and the date widget as a Sunny card, over a dark wallpaper and over a striped test wallpaper, where the bend can be seen following the lobes; the Shape choice in the widget's options; the glass count badge on My feed.
- The Style setting found by settings search, in the minified build.

**On an emulator:** the control panel at Expressive, with the torch tile on (accent colour, rounded square), the ringer's chosen mode and the thick slider track.

**In unit tests only (1,450 pass; lint clean):** what each level means, the control and tile shapes, the readable tonal colours, which widgets may take a shape, the backup round trip.

**Not seen on a phone:**
- How smoothly Home pages with a shaped glass widget on it. An ordinary glass widget already costs some smoothness there (Appearance › Widgets says so), and a shaped one is drawn the same live way.
- Uno widgets other than the clock and the date in a shape, or with the display numerals.
- Sunny in glass (it was seen as a card), and the press and tile shape changes in motion.
- The control panel on the phone itself.
