# Uno Launcher 0.138.0-uno01

Live scores on the island, a Home that learns, more shapes for widgets and icons, and more the assistant can do without a model.

## What's changed

### Island

- **Live scores.** When a sports app has its live-score notification up, the island shows it as a scoreboard: one team's mark and score to the left of the camera and the other's to the right, and on the card each team's mark and name, the scores large, and where the game is. Uno fetches nothing itself: it reads that notification's title and text for a score ("Arsenal 2 - 1 Chelsea", "LAL 98, BOS 101 · Q3 4:21") and leaves anything else as it was. A team's mark is its short name on a roundel of its own colour; the launcher carries no club badges. On by default; **Island › Live Scores** turns it off.
- **While Qwen thinks,** the pill and its card show Material's shape-morphing loader, turning, at Balanced and Expressive (the loader search shows for the same moment). When it is done, and at Calm, it is a sparkle; it was the feed's glyph.

### Home

- **Suggested apps,** a new widget (4 × 2 or 2 × 2): the apps you usually open about now, then the ones you open most. A tap opens one. Hidden apps, and apps Focus is hiding, are never shown.
- **Smart Rotate** for a widget stack (in its options): as Home comes to the front, the stack turns to the widget you usually look at about now. A turn by hand always wins.
- **Both learn only if you let them.** **Home Screen › Learn from How You Use Home** (off until you turn it on, or set a stack to Smart Rotate) counts which apps you open from Uno in each hour, and which widget of a stack you turn to when: 24 fading numbers each, on this phone, in no backup, forgotten with one tap in the same place. Until then Suggested apps shows the apps you open most, which the launcher has always kept for search.
- **More widget shapes:** Clover, Flower, Burst and Pentagon join Cookie and Sunny (a widget's options, with Style at Balanced or Expressive). The Photos widget can take a shape too, its picture filling it. The four new shapes in Glass are glass without the bend along the edge.
- **Icon Shape** (Appearance › App Icons; Default unless you change it): cut every app's icon to a Cookie, Clover, Flower, Sunny or Pentagon, or with Mixed give each app its own. Icon lighting and the shadow follow the shape. An icon pack's icons keep their own outlines.

### Assistant

Three things Qwen now does at once, with no model asked (with **Let Qwen do tasks** on). They need no new Uno Lab and no retraining: the launcher recognises them itself, and only when the whole request is one it understands.

- **Reminders:** "remind me to call Sam at 5", "remind me in 20 minutes to take the bread out". The clock app opens with an alarm named for what it is about, and you tap Save; the clock app keeps and rings it. "At 5" is the next 5 there is. A reminder for another day still goes to the planner's calendar.
- **Uno's Style:** "use the expressive style", "set the style to calm", with Undo.
- **Uno's settings:** "open uno settings", "open the launcher's keyboard settings".

## Notes: what this has and hasn't been tested on

**On an emulator, with the test build (Uno Dev):** an ongoing notification reading "Arsenal 2 - 1 Chelsea", posted as an app would post it, came through the notification listener and drew the scoreboard pill; a ride's "Arriving at 5:42 PM" was left as it was. The scoreboard card, the pill and card while the assistant works, Flower and Clover widgets, and icons cut to Cookie (with lighting, in the dock too) were seen. Device tests cover the Suggested apps widget, the scoreboard and the assistant's glyph.

1,508 unit tests pass, lint is clean, and 19 device tests pass. Among them: every phrase the planner is evaluated on is left to the planner by the new recognisers, the planner's catalogue is pinned by name and hash, and nineteen things that are not scores (clock times, gates, orders, downloads) are refused.

**Not tested against a real sports app.** Which apps post a live score as an ongoing notification, and how each words it, is not known; an app whose score is an ordinary alert, or is worded another way, will not show. A score in a notification Android itself has promoted to a Live Update was not seen on a device.

**Not seen on any screen:** the Photos widget in a shape; Burst and Pentagon; any new shape in the Glass style; Mixed icons; icon shapes with the Themed, Tinted or Dark icon styles, or on a work app's badge; the Suggested apps widget and Smart Rotate on a real Home; a reminder's alarm in a clock app. Nothing in this release was seen on a phone.
