# Uno Launcher 0.132.6-uno01

Smoother swipes and search, answers that fall into place letter by letter, and search that closes on a spring.

## What's changed

- **Smoother Home swipes.** The island's glass no longer follows the wallpaper drift while it is closed, so it stops redrawing the whole page on every frame of a swipe. Late frames on a Pixel 10a (60 Hz):

  | Interaction | Before | After |
  |---|---|---|
  | Swiping Home pages | 26.6% | 2.6–3.0% |
  | Into and out of the App Library | 19.4% | 4.7% |
  | Search over an app (open, close) | ~1% | ~1% |
  | Opening an app and returning | ~3% | ~3% |
  | Ask Qwen's streamed answer over an app | n/a | 1.65% |

- **Answers fall into place.** Ask Qwen's answer now appears letter by letter at reading pace, speeding up only when it falls behind the model. Each letter drops gently into place and arrives in a soft rainbow, then settles into the text colour over about 1.4 seconds, so the colour trails across the newest few words. A word is shown only once it is complete, so nothing wraps and jumps back. The colours depend only on a letter's place and age, so nothing flickers. While Qwen is writing, the card's edge has a slow, faint turning glow. An answer you come back to plays through quickly instead of appearing all at once.
- **Streamed answers are cheaper.** Pieces from Qwen are gathered into one update per frame, and the answer no longer restarts the results card's resize spring for every word.
- **Search closes on a spring.** Closing search, on Home or over an app, now settles on a critically damped spring. Swiping it away carries the swipe's speed into the close.
- **The island's "answer ready" opens over the app you're in.** When the island says Qwen's answer is ready, tapping it now shows the answer over whatever is on screen, instead of taking you to Home.
- **Not above the lock screen.** The assist gesture no longer opens search over the lock screen. It asks you to unlock first, then opens search.
- **Search opens faster.** Where a search can be continued (stores, the browser) is found in the background as search opens, not while you type.

## Notes: what this has and hasn't been tested on

**On the Pixel 10a** (GrapheneOS, Android 17, Uno Lab 0.3.8), with signed builds from along the way:
- The numbers above, measured with `dumpsys gfxinfo` over ten repeats of each gesture.
- An earlier, word-by-word version of the reveal.

**On an emulator only** (the final reveal and the island tap):
- The letter-by-letter reveal, recorded at Qwen's real speed: no flashes or missing letters.
- Spotlight's 11 device tests.
- 1,213 unit tests pass (1 skipped); lint clean.

**Not seen:**
- This exact build on the phone, including the letter-by-letter reveal with the real model.
- Tapping the island's "answer ready" over an app.
- The assist gesture on the lock screen.

**Known:** opening search on Home is still GPU-bound (13–20% late frames), because the panel re-renders Home behind it. This is next.
