# Uno Launcher 0.136.0-uno01

A keyboard for fast typing: no dead strips between keys, overlapping fingers typed in order, sharper haptics and key cards that are always over their key. And an assistant log with a thumbs-up and thumbs-down on every response.

## What's changed

### Keyboard

- **Every touch on the keys types.** The strips between the rows typed nothing: 15% of the key area, measured. Each key's touch now reaches to the middle of the gap round it, and the keys at the ends of a row reach the keyboard's edge. The keys are drawn exactly where they were.
- **Overlapping fingers are typed in order.** The space bar types when it is let go (so a slide can move the cursor), and in fast typing the next letter lands before the thumb has left it: the letter came out before the space. A key that is waiting now acts the moment another key is touched.
- **Shift is a modifier while held.** Hold it and type: capitals, and it turns off when you let go. Shift and the 123, ABC and #+= keys act as they are touched, not when released. Two taps still lock caps.
- **A symbol no longer sends you back to the letters.** The numbers and symbols pages stay until you leave them.
- **Web address fields have the ordinary bottom row,** with the full space bar. A browser's address bar is where searches are typed, and `/`, `.` and `.com` beside the space bar left it too small. Email fields keep `@` and `.`.
- **Key cards are always over their key.** With two fingers down, a key could be left blank with no card, and a card could sit over a key that had changed under it. There is one card, for the latest key touched; a key hides only while its card is up; the card stays at least long enough to be seen on the quickest tap.
- **Every key answers a touch.** In the Classic style, letter keys without cards and the space bar showed nothing when pressed. Pressed looks come on with the touch and fade as it lifts. The suggestion slots and the buttons above the keys answer as they are touched too.
- **Crisper haptics.** Letters, Delete and the space bar now play the motor's shortest tick; on a Pixel 10a that is over in about 35 ms, against 41 for the low tick letters used and 68 for the click the space bar used. Shift and the page keys are one firm tick, not two close together. **Soft** in Keyboard settings is the feel the keys had before; **Firm** is a click.
- **A tap on the line between two keys is read as the one the word wants.** Only a tap in the gap between two keys or on the very edge they share, only when the word so far goes on to much commoner words with the other key, only in ordinary text with Autocorrect on, and never for the key straight after Delete. A tap on a key's face is always that key.
- A Return that sends, searches or goes is called off, not fired, if another key lands while it is held.

### Assistant log

- **Keep an assistant log** (Settings › Search, off until you switch it on). While it is on, every response from Qwen is written down whole: what you said, the facts from the phone that went with it, exactly what the model wrote, each tool it used with its values and what it did, what you were shown, and how it ended (including a plan you turned down, an answer cut short by a follow-up, a failure, and whether you used Undo).
- **Thumbs-up and thumbs-down** appear under each response in search, and under past turns in the conversation: up for one that acted correctly or helpfully, down for one that was not what you meant. A second tap takes it back. The verdict is saved into that response's entry.
- **Save log to a file** writes it as JSON lines, one response a line, wherever you choose. **Delete log** removes it.
- The log is in the launcher's private storage, in no Android backup or device transfer, and not in Uno's settings backup (nor is its switch). It leaves the phone only through Save log to a file. It is separate from your conversations: deleting those does not delete it. The newest 2,000 responses are kept.

## Notes: what this has and hasn't been tested on

**On an emulator, with the real keyboard typing into a real field:** letters, space, the 123 key and symbols (the page stayed); "th" then a tap in the middle of W typed "thw", a tap on W's very edge beside E typed "the", and a tap just under W stayed W.

**In device tests on an emulator:** a tap at every point of a grid over the keys types the nearest key, in two keyboard arrangements; a letter, Return and a suggestion each come after a space bar still held; Shift held and tapped; every character key's card in five arrangements (default; number row, one-handed and Classic; numbers; symbols in an email field at a taller key height; QWERTZ with caps lock); swiping a word; the alternatives strip, also one-handed; the assistant log's file, thumbs and off state.

**On a Pixel 10a, with the test build (Uno Dev) and Uno Lab 0.3.10:** the key card over a held key and the page keys in the settings page's keyboard; an answer and a planned task ("set a timer for 5 minutes", planned and not run) logged with the planner's reply and its tool, the thumbs on both, a rating still there after the launcher was stopped and reopened.

**Not felt:** the haptics. Their lengths were read from the phone's vibrator log, but nobody has typed on them yet; if Crisp is not to your taste, Soft is the old feel.

**Not seen:** the Classic style's new pressed colours; the keyboard in landscape; the real keyboard in a web address field (the browser on the emulator would not start; the row itself is unit-tested); a task that ran its tools, or Undo, on the phone (unit-tested).

1,478 unit tests pass and lint is clean.

**Not logged:** greetings answered from the launcher's own table (no model is asked), and what a suggested action did after it was tapped (only that it was tapped).
