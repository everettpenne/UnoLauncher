# Uno Launcher 0.33.0-uno01

Notification cards, downloads and navigation in the island, with spoiler protection and per-app timeouts.

## What's new

- **Notification cards.** A new notification drops the island down into a card that tucks away after a few seconds. In the island settings choose how much it shows: the app's name only (the default), plus the title, or plus the text. Tap the card to open the notification.
- **Spoiler protection.** List words that keep a notification's text off the island; a match shows only the app's name.
- **Per-app timeouts.** Choose how long each app's card stays (3, 6, 10 or 15 seconds).
- **Downloads and other progress** show as a ring round the app's icon with a percentage.
- **Navigation** shows the next instruction and the distance, from any maps app that posts a navigation notification.

## Good to know

Android's own pop-up banner still appears as well, because a launcher cannot suppress it. To see only the island's card, turn off "pop on screen" for those apps in Android's notification settings. Cards and the new activities all follow the "Apps that can use the island" list and have their own switches. Inline reply is not included, because the island over other apps cannot take typing.
