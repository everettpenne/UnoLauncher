# Uno Launcher 0.65.0-uno01

A smoother island, and more on it: a review of the Dynamic Island as it behaves on a Pixel 10a (a small round camera at the top centre), with the fixes and additions that came out of it.

## What's new

- **Smoother open and close.** The island no longer recomposes on every frame of its morph, and its open panel is laid out once at its final size and revealed by the growing body, instead of re-wrapping its text each frame. A test holds a whole open or close to a handful of compositions (the old code used about one per frame).
- **The open panel is tall enough.** With a feed headline showing, the Search / Feed / Settings row was cut off the bottom, and the headline lost its second line. The headline's room is now counted, and so is a larger system font. Island text uses its font's own line height, so rows are as tall as their text.
- **Next alarm** beside the date on the open island ("7:30 AM", or with the weekday when more than a day away). It reads the system's alarm clock and needs no permission.
- **Song times** on the seek bar: time played on the left, time left on the right.
- **Screen reader support.** The island is a button that states whether it is open, says what is live on it, and announces a new event or notification card once.
- **Reduced motion.** With Android's animations off, the island snaps instead of springing.
- **Includes 0.64.0**, which was never published on its own: Apple's HIG / SF Pro type scale, set in Inter, across the launcher (see the changelog).

## Notes: what this has and hasn't been tested on

Unit tests pass, and the island's instrumented tests (including the new smoothness and panel-height tests) pass on an Android 16 emulator at a 1080 x 2400 screen with a small round camera, close to the Pixel 10a. **This release has not been run on a real phone.** The emulator cannot say how smooth the morph feels (it draws without a real GPU), so the smoothness change is held to the number of compositions, not to frame times. Worth checking on the Pixel: opening and closing with music playing, with the feed headline on, and the Search / Feed / Settings row at the bottom of the open island. See `docs/device-checklist.md`.
