# Uno Launcher 0.127.3-uno01

A more polished island: it lands exactly, its contents grow with it, the charging ring slides under it, and search hands back to it as one shape.

## What's changed

- **The island lands exactly**: no more jump of the last few pixels at the end of every open and close, and the close lands crisply instead of creeping.
- **Its contents grow with it**: what the open island shows appears early and grows with the body, instead of waiting behind an empty black slab and coming through a heavy blur. The handle fades in with it.
- **The ring slides under the island** as it opens and back out as it closes, instead of vanishing and reappearing round a still-large body.
- **Search hands back to the island as one shape**, and opens 30 % more slowly than the island, since it grows several times as far.
- The open island keeps "N min to full" while it closes.
- Includes everything in 0.127.2-uno01: a slower, smoother island, Home staying still behind it, and the charging ring behind the island.
