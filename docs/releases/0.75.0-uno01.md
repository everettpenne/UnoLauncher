# Uno Launcher 0.75.0-uno01

Settings reorganized around you, a Spotlight that does more of the typing, and a fresher island.

## What's new

- **Settings, reorganized.** Thirteen destinations became nine goal-oriented pages: Appearance (wallpaper, glass, icons), Home layout, The island, Notifications & privacy (access → badges → cards → Focus → hidden apps → permission ledger), Gestures & feel (panel tiles now live next to the swipe that opens them), Search, News feed, Keyboard, and Backup & about. Every switch is two taps away; no meaning, default or storage changed.
- **Spotlight does more of the typing.** Bare numbers offer Dial, domains offer "Open in browser", an empty search offers one-tap Stopwatch/Torch/Focus, phone numbers are no longer mistaken for sums, and the apps you actually use outrank better-spelled ones you never open.
- **A fresher island.** A Live Update that hasn't updated in ten minutes steps off the pill (navigation and countdowns stay fresh); its card still waits on the open island.
