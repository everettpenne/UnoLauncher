# Uno Launcher 0.16.12-uno01

The island pill no longer runs to the top of the screen and its size slider changes its height, and the page-dots strip no longer overlaps the feed and Discover pages.

## What's new

- **Island pill sized from the real camera hole.** Android reports the cutout as a bounding rectangle that is often much taller than the visible hole and starts at the top edge. The pill is now sized from the cutout's own outline, keeps 6 dp clear of the top of the screen, and the **Island size** slider sweeps its height across the whole range that fits instead of doing nothing.
- **Page dots no longer overlap pages.** Every page now reserves exactly the height of the bottom strip (48 dp, or 96 dp while the "Set as home app" button is showing) instead of guessing, including the feed page and the native Google Discover window.
- **Layout check tool.** `scripts/size-matrix.sh` screenshots Home and the feed page across phone, Fold cover, small-phone, large-text, Fold inner and tablet sizes on one emulator, so layouts can be checked without owning a foldable.

## Notes

- The pill height is sized from the cutout outline that Android reports. It was checked on emulator cutouts (a corner hole and a centered notch) and in unit tests that assume a Pixel 10a-like hole; it has not yet been confirmed on a physical Pixel 10a. If the pill still looks off on your phone, the output of `adb shell dumpsys window displays | grep mDisplayCutout` pins the exact geometry.
- This release is validated on emulators; treat it as a beta and report problems with your device model and Android version.
