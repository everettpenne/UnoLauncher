# Uno Launcher 0.123.0-uno01

iOS's continuous corners everywhere, a keyboard that matches the launcher, and every emoji.

## What's changed

- **Corners**: every panel, sheet, menu, widget, dock, the island and every glass surface has iOS's continuous corner instead of a circular arc. Nested shapes follow what holds them (docks with their icons, a sheet's bottom with the display's corners, cards with their panel), and every app icon and icon tile has the same squircle.
- **Edge Highlight**: Appearance → Glass → Edge Highlight turns the rim light off on all glass, the keyboard's included.
- **Keyboard look**: the same corners as the launcher, its accent (Uno orange or your wallpaper's) instead of iOS blue, and its edge light.
- **Every emoji**: all 1,906 of Unicode Emoji 16.0, every flag among them, searchable, with skin tones on a long press. Any your phone cannot draw is hidden.
- **Text Replacement**: type a shortcut (omw is built in) and press space for its phrase; Delete puts the shortcut back. Manage them in Settings → Keyboard.
- **Address fields**: @ and . beside the space bar for email; /, . and .com for web addresses.
- **More room above Android's keyboard switcher**, so typing on the bottom row no longer hits it.
