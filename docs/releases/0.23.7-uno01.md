# Uno Launcher 0.23.7-uno01

A bigger island: two live activities at once, camera and microphone indicators, an event queue, gestures, album-art colour and a smoother open and close. It also fixes the pause icon and waveform sticking in "playing".

## What's fixed

- **Pause icon and waveform.** After a pause the icon stayed on "pause" and the bars kept moving. Android was asked "is music playing?" at the moment of the change, and right after a pause it can still say yes; nothing asked again. The island now asks again a moment later and after every button press, the icon flips as soon as you tap, and the bars go dim and still while a paused player can be resumed.

## What's new

- **Two live activities at once.** The pill carries up to two things, one on each side of the camera: camera or microphone in use, a call, a timer, a stopwatch, music. Flick sideways across the island to bring a different one to the front.
- **Camera and microphone indicators.** A green camera mark and an orange microphone mark show while any app is using that hardware, like Android's own dots. They need no permission, Android does not say which app, and nothing is stored. Turn them off under Notifications.
- **Events wait their turn.** If a second event arrives (Silent, then Airplane), it waits behind the first instead of replacing it.
- **Gestures.** Swipe up to close the island or dismiss a banner, flick sideways to send a banner away or turn the live activities. There is no swipe down: Android uses a downward drag from the top of the screen for the notification shade, so the island opens with a tap or a long-press as before.
- **Album-art colour.** The bars and the play button take a colour from the artwork when notification access provides it.
- **Smoother.** Opening and closing crossfade instead of swapping halfway, closing settles onto the camera without overshooting, and the panel stays open longer while music plays. Over other apps the island's window is no longer resized on every animation frame, which had clipped its corners. The clock is gone from the idle pill.

## Notes: what this has and hasn't been tested on

On an emulator, over Chrome with the accessibility overlay: tap and long-press open the island, tap outside and swipe up close it; a test page that opens the camera and microphone lit both marks, and a flick swapped which was in front; Silent followed by Airplane showed one after the other; the pause icon flipped and the bars dimmed straight after a tap. The planning of the two slots, the event queue and the artwork tint are unit tested. The emulator is slow and its camera sits at the screen's left edge, so the layout on a centred camera is untested.

Not tested on your phone, so please check:

- **Pause with NewPipe** still works, and the icon stays on "play" afterwards. On the emulator Chrome ignored the audio-focus pause, so the icon went back to "pause" when the audio was still playing; that is the island showing the truth, and NewPipe was fine in 0.23.6.
- **The two slots with a centred camera**: a timer and music together, the camera mark, the flick.
- **The collapse and expand animations**: no clipped corners, no jump onto the camera.
- **Notification peeks queue**: two notifications in a row show one after the other.
