# Uno Launcher 0.125.0-uno01

The island takes Apple's shape, splits like a drop, and can rest at the camera alone.

## What's changed

- **Squircle pill**: the resting island's ends are squircle halves instead of semicircles, as Apple's are. They ease back to round as it opens.
- **Concentric open island**: its corners follow the phone's own screen corners (the screen's corner less the island's margin from the top).
- **Liquid split**: a second activity pulls out of the pill as a drop on a neck that stretches thin and pinches off, and melts back in the same way.
- **Pill at Rest**: in Island settings, on by default. Off, the island is only a ring round the camera until it has something to show.
- **Search by description** (from 0.124.0-uno01, which was tagged but not published): Spotlight and the App Library match an app's own one-line description as well as its name.
- Includes everything in 0.123.0-uno01: continuous corners everywhere, Edge Highlight, a keyboard that matches the launcher, every emoji, Text Replacement and address-field keys.

## Notes: what this has and hasn't been tested on

1,155 unit tests pass. Seen on an emulator: the squircle pill, Pill at Rest in settings and its preview, and a capsule merging back into the pill on its neck. **Not seen:** any of it on a phone; the ring round a real camera; the neck pinching off on the way out (too quick for the emulator's screenshots); the concentric corner (the emulator reports no display corner); descriptions in Spotlight or the App Library.
