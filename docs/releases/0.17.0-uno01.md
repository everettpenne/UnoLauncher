# Uno Launcher 0.17.0-uno01

A control panel of its own, island tools, Focus, themed icons, and opt-in notification badges and contact search.

## What's new

- **Control panel.** Swipe down on the right side of Home for a glass panel with media, volume, brightness, ringer, flashlight, Focus and up to five app shortcuts, plus a row that hands off to Android's real Quick Settings. Show, hide and reorder the tiles under **Customize > Control panel & extras**, or switch the swipe back to Android's Quick Settings there. Notifications still come from the left side.
- **Better sliders.** A haptic tick per volume step, and a little rubber-band stretch at the ends.
- **Focus.** Pick apps to hide from Home and All apps while Focus is on; nothing is moved or uninstalled. Optionally set the ringer to vibrate with it.
- **Island tools.** Long-press the island for a timer (1, 5, 10, 30 minutes), a stopwatch and the flashlight. A timer counts down in the collapsed island and rings until you tap it.
- **Themed icons.** Each app's own single-colour icon layer in the launcher's colours (Android 13+), with other apps washed to match.
- **Opt-in notification access:** badges on icons, the playing track's title and artwork, and an island peek showing only the app's name. Message text is never read.
- **Opt-in contact search** in All apps (names only).
- Folders on glass get edge-lit tiles and badges.

## Permissions

Nothing new is asked for at install beyond Vibrate. Brightness needs "Modify system settings", Silent needs Do Not Disturb access, the to-the-second timer needs exact alarms, contact search needs Contacts, and badges, track titles and the peek need notification access. Each is requested only when you use that feature and is easy to deny; the launcher works fully without them. See PRIVACY.md.

## Notes: what this release has and hasn't been tested on

This build has only run on an emulator, and that emulator renders in software (frames take seconds), so it threw "not responding" dialogs during heavier flows (typing in All apps search, long sessions). The published 0.16.14 does the same in the same flow, so I believe that is the emulator, but it means performance on real hardware is unmeasured. If you see jank, turn off **Liquid glass**.

Verified on the emulator: the panel opens and its volume, ringer and brightness controls change real system state; Silent without Do Not Disturb access opens Android's access screen; Focus hides apps and leaves the layout alone; shortcuts show in the panel; themed icons render; badges draw (counts were injected, since the emulator has no app posting notifications); the island tools open, the timer counts down, fires on time and shows "Timer done"; the notification listener binds; the contact query returns a test contact.

Not verified, so please try these on your Pixel:

- **Flashlight** (the emulator has no flash).
- **Playback detection, the transport buttons, and track title and artwork** (no audio on the emulator).
- **Real notification badges and the island peek** from real apps, and how they behave with work-profile apps.
- **Contact search results on screen**, and tapping a result.
- **Haptics** (the volume ticks and the slider stretch).
- **The timer ringing and vibrating with the screen off**, and whether tapping the island silences it.
- **Back and swipe-up dismissal** of the control panel.
- Real-hardware performance and battery.

This is a beta; report problems with your device model and Android version.
