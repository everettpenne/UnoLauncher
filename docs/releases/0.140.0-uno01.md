# Uno Launcher 0.140.0-uno01

Bolder Hyper Glass icons, with two clear styles that bend the wallpaper; Hyper Glass Cards, an experimental look for the feed, the App Library, menus and search; settings put in order; and the end of a scroll no longer stutters.

## What's changed

### Hyper Glass icons (experimental)

- **Bolder.** The picture fills more of the glass (a one-colour drawing 94 % of it, where it was 78 %; an app's own picture about a quarter larger) and the edge bends it harder and from further in. This changes the four styles from 0.139.0 as well.
- **Two clear styles.** **Hyper Glass Clear** (the picture in white) and **Hyper Glass Clear Color** (the app's own colours) have no card: on Home's pages the wallpaper shows through each icon, bent by the same edge, and moves under it as the pages do. The dock's icons, and icons anywhere else (the App Library, search, folders), are the see-through picture without the bend.
- **The app's own colours, two ways.** **Hyper Glass Color** (on black, from 0.139.0) and **Hyper Glass Clear Color** (on clear glass). Both are in **Appearance › App Icons › Icon Style**; the list is longer than the menu is tall, so scroll it to reach them.
- **How clear glass is kept cheap.** An icon's own layer never changes, so it is made once as a picture, like every icon style. Only the wallpaper seen through it is drawn live: one small program shared by every icon, two readings a pixel, from the picture of the wallpaper Home already keeps for its glass. Keeping each icon in a layer of its own was tried and was several times slower.

### Hyper Glass Cards (experimental)

A new switch, off unless you turn it on: **Appearance › Glass › Hyper Glass Cards**. The feed, the App Library, pop-up and long-press menus and the search results turn from frosted glass to a nearly clear pane:

- the wallpaper behind, blurred, with very little painted over it;
- a fixed film grain over the whole pane;
- a fine border split into colour, red to one side and blue to the other;
- a slow drift of the picture behind, stepped a few times a second and only while a card is on screen (at rest on Home nothing is drawn for it);
- as Home's pages move, the border's colours part further, and come back as the pages land.

The settings sheet, folders, the control panel and the widget picker stay frosted. Text on a card is not split into colour. Needs glass on and Android 13 or later; the switch is not shown otherwise.

### Settings, put in order

- **Appearance › App Icons** holds icon style, shape, lighting and icon packs.
- **Island** keeps its main switches, Line Up and Style, and gains three pages: **What It Shows**, **Notifications** and **Widget & Apps**. It was one scroll of about fifteen groups.
- **Assistant** is a page of its own (it was at the bottom of Search, which links to it).
- **Privacy** is a page of its own: the camera and microphone log, hidden apps and the permission ledger. What it shared a page with is now **Notifications & Focus**.
- The first page is grouped: Appearance, Home Screen, Island; Search, Assistant, Keyboard, News Feed; Notifications & Focus, Privacy, Gestures; General.

Nothing was renamed underneath: every setting keeps what you chose, and the settings search goes to the page each is on now.

### Fixes

- **The end of a scroll no longer stutters.** A fling that reached the end of a page fell through to Android's stretch effect, which redraws the whole page of glass through a filter on every frame, and a drag back from past the end scrolled the list while the page was still stretched, so letting go made it jump. Flings now bounce on the launcher's own rubber band, a drag back lets the band go slack first, Android's stretch is off throughout the launcher, and the settings sheet has the band too.

## Privacy

Nothing new is read, kept or sent. The clear icons and the cards draw from the picture of your wallpaper the launcher already keeps in memory for its glass.

## Notes: what this has and hasn't been tested on

Everything here was tested on an emulator (Android 16) with the test build (Uno Dev). **None of it has been run on a phone.**

**Clear glass icons:** seen on Home in both clear styles and in mixed icon shapes, and chosen from the settings by hand. How smoothly a full page of them swipes was measured once, cleanly, on the emulator with an earlier, slightly heavier version of the program: a page of 24 clear icons took about a third longer to draw a frame than plain icons (23 ms against 17 ms, median, in a debug build). An emulator's graphics say little about a phone's, so on a Pixel this may be invisible or may not be. **Not tested:** how white see-through icons read on light panels (the App Library and search in the light theme).

**Hyper Glass Cards:** seen on the feed, the App Library, a long-press menu, a settings dropdown and the search results, in light and dark, and held to a device test of the grain and the split border. The drift's cost at rest was counted: 3 frames drawn in 4 seconds on Home, about 12 a second on the App Library. **Not tested:** a dark wallpaper under the light theme, or a bright one under the dark theme, where so little fill is most likely to cost the text its contrast.

**The end of a scroll:** a fling into the bottom of the settings sheet gave one smooth bounce and came to rest. The App Library and the feed use the same code and were not recorded.

**Settings:** the new pages were opened; the settings search and the pages' order have unit tests.

1,536 unit tests pass and lint is clean. Of the device tests run on the emulator for this release, three fail, as they do on 0.139.0: two glass-menu tests and one that looks for a line of help text. The full device suite was not run to the end.
