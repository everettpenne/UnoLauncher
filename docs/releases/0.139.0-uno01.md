# Uno Launcher 0.139.0-uno01

Hyper Glass, an experimental icon style; a wave of colour when the assistant finishes; and live scores that read theScore, with real team badges and favourite teams.

## What's changed

### Hyper Glass icons (experimental)

Four new choices of **Icon Style** (Appearance, App Icons): **Hyper Glass** (white on black), **Hyper Glass Light** (dark on white), **Hyper Glass Accent** (white on your accent) and **Hyper Glass Color** (the app's own colours on black).

- **The picture is inside the glass.** Each app's icon has its plate taken off (an adaptive icon's foreground alone; for an older flat icon, the colour round its edge cleared inwards), and what is left is bent by the glass's own edge: within a thick rim, each point looks inwards across the edge, so the middle of the picture is untouched and the rim gathers the picture into soft pools, most of all in the corners. A blue haze follows the pools, with a narrow blue light and a fine white line on the edge.
- **In one colour or the app's own.** The one-colour styles use the app's own themed drawing where it has one, and otherwise the picture's light and dark.
- **In every icon shape.** Default, Cookie, Clover, Flower, Sunny, Pentagon and Mixed: the bend follows each outline.
- **Made once, like any icon style.** Icons are drawn once and kept, so the style costs nothing while you use Home and is the same on every Android version. An icon pack's icons are left as they are.

It is an experiment. Not yet on Home: the bend deepening as you press an icon, and clear glass with no card that bends the wallpaper behind it. Both exist and are tested, in a debug build's gallery only.

### Assistant

- **A wave when it finishes.** When an answer or a task finishes, one wave of split colour crosses the whole screen from the island and is gone in under a second. Once per turn; not for a failure, a question back, or an answer that finished while you were elsewhere. None with Android's animations off, or on Android 12. It is strong: at its height the text under it is split three ways for a moment.

### Island

- **theScore's live games are read.** 0.138.0 read a score from a notification's title and text. theScore's live game has neither: the app draws the whole card itself (each team's badge, "MIL", "19", "1st", "5:37", "TOR", "18"), so the island showed nothing. For an ongoing notification with no title and no text, Uno now lays that card out in memory, as the notification shade does, and reads the words on it. They are a score only when exactly two of them are whole numbers with a team's name beside each; anything else is left alone and nothing from it is shown or kept.
- **Real team badges.** When the card has two pictures, they are the two teams' badges, and the pill and the scoreboard card show them in place of Uno's coloured roundels. They are the sports app's own pictures from its own notification, shown only while it is up. Uno still fetches nothing.
- **Favourite Teams** (Island, under Shows, while Live Scores is on): with more than one game live, a game with one of your teams leads on the pill. Write a team the way the scoreboard does ("MIN") or by its name ("Minnesota"; "Timberwolves" where the app writes names in full), separated by commas. A scoreboard that shows only "MIN" cannot be matched to "Timberwolves".
- **Two live games keep their places.** Each posts again every few seconds, and the newest post used to lead, so the pill would swap between them. Games are now ordered by when each was first seen (after your favourites).
- **"MIN" is a team.** A scoreboard's capitals are read as a team even when they spell a counting word; Minnesota was being refused as "minutes".

### Fixes

- App changes (an install, an update, a removal) are delivered to the launcher on the main thread, said outright. Nothing changed for a person using the launcher; it stopped 121 device tests failing before they began.

## Privacy

One more thing is read from a notification, and `PRIVACY.md` says so: for a live score with no title or text, the words and pictures on the card its app drew. It is read in memory, only with Live Scores on, only for an ongoing notification that is not a conversation, a call, an email or a transfer, and used only if the words are a score. Hyper Glass and the wave read nothing new: the icons are the ones the launcher already loads.

## Notes: what this has and hasn't been tested on

**Hyper Glass, on an emulator (Android 16) with the test build (Uno Dev):** all four styles were set and looked at on Home and in the dock. The glass itself has unit tests of its sums and device tests that hold the live shader against them in every icon shape. **Not tested:** on a phone; with an icon pack; with hundreds of apps (each icon is made once, off the main thread, as other styles are; how long a first load takes was not measured); the settings row tapped by hand (the style was set in the settings file).

**The wave, on the same emulator:** seen on a real answer from Qwen (Uno Lab 0.3.10 with the 2B model) and held still in device tests, which also check that it fires once, for a task as well as an answer, and not for an answer that finished off screen. **Not tested:** on a phone; over another app, where the code is the same but it was never watched; how smooth it is (the emulator recorded only a few frames of it).

**Live scores, on a Pixel 10a with the test build, during two live NBA games in theScore (23 November 2026):** both games were read from theScore's own notifications with both badges each, the pill held steady, the scoreboard card showed the badges, the scores and the period and clock, and setting a favourite put that team's game first each time. **Not tested:** any sports app other than theScore; a game that ends or goes to overtime while it is up. A dark badge is low in contrast on the black pill.

1,528 unit tests pass and lint is clean. The assistant's device tests pass on the emulator. The full device suite was not run to the end for this release: it is slow and has failures on the emulator that are older than these changes (among them two glass-menu tests and one island test, which fail the same way without them).
