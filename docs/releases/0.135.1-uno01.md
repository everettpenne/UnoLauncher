# Uno Launcher 0.135.1-uno01

Fixes from a sweep of every screen on a phone: text you can read over any wallpaper, a page indicator that keeps its icon, and nine smaller things.

## What's changed

- **Settings read over any wallpaper.** Orange actions ("Add Widget", "Choose Apps", "Replace"), check marks and the red "Remove Photo" nearly vanished where a vivid wallpaper showed through the settings sheet. Accent-coloured words are now deepened (lifted, in the dark theme) until they read, and each group of rows sits on a calmer, more opaque plate.
- **The page indicator keeps its icon.** After scrolling My feed or the App Library, the selected marker at the bottom went blank until you changed page.
- **"What's new" is about this version.** After updating to 0.134.0 or 0.135.0 the card listed changes from 0.132 and 0.133. (It is shown once per series, so it will not appear again if you already dismissed it in 0.135.0.)
- **The Lock Screen clock widget can be read.** With widgets set to Card, the default, it was white type on a white card. It sits straight on the wallpaper again.
- **Shapes only where they work.** The Liquid battery in a Cookie or Sunny shape drew as a small rectangle inside it. Widgets that paint to their edge (Liquid battery, Photos, the Lock Screen clock) keep their rounded square, and Shape is not offered for them.
- **Buttons on card widgets can be read.** "Allow" on the Screen time widget was dark on dark over a dark wallpaper.
- **Search over Home leaves Home alone.** Opening search no longer lifts the page indicator and search button above the keyboard, where they sat on top of widgets.
- **App Library headers scroll with their rows** on glass. Pinned to the top, the rows passing under a header showed round its badge.
- **Home Screen settings name your widgets** ("Clock", "Battery"), not only "2 × 2 · Row 1".
- **Clearer names in Appearance:** Style (the Calm to Expressive dial), Icon Style, and Mode (light and dark). There were two rows called Style and one called Appearance.
- **Calm means one font.** With Style at Calm the island's headline is in the launcher's own font too; your headline font is kept for when you leave Calm.
- Two widget lines that were cut short: the media widget's hint when it needs notification access, and the Day, month and year widget's title.

## Notes: what this has and hasn't been tested on

**On a Pixel 10a, with the test build (Uno Dev):** each fix above was seen before and after, on a vivid test wallpaper and a dark one, with all twenty Uno widgets on Home at Calm, Balanced and Expressive, except the two below.

**Not seen on the phone:** the island headline's font at Calm (unit-tested), and the new "What's new" text (the card had already been dismissed on the test phone). 1,453 unit tests pass and lint is clean.

**Not covered by the sweep:** the island on the phone itself (the installed launcher's island covers the test build's), larger system text sizes, landscape, and the dark theme. The project's instrumented tests were not run.
