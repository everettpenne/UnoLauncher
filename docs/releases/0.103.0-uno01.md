# Uno Launcher 0.103.0-uno01

A polish pass across Home, and a sensible island width.

## What's changed

- **The island at rest is a sensible width again**, centred on the camera when that costs no more than 8 dp a side. **Uno's status bar is removed.**
- **Widget stacks** show small dots at their edge only while you change widgets, instead of a dark pill over the widget.
- **Glass** has a fine, light-catching edge instead of an outline. **Icon names** are plain white with a soft shadow, with a halo only over bright wallpaper.
- **Icons**: small logos fill their tile, old round icons sit in the iOS shape, and a new **Dark** icon style (Launcher settings › App icons). Icon lighting adds a soft shadow.
- **Consistency**: one type scale and one spacing scale, and the glass kit's buttons throughout sheets and settings.
- **Motion**: apps open out of their icon from folders and search too; folders, search, the control panel and widget stacks share one set of springs.
