# Uno Launcher 0.23.10-uno01

A new look and more features for Uno Keyboard: closer to iOS, with an emoji panel, a Paste button, one-handed mode and symbol variants, and fixes for two ways it could disappear while typing.

## What's new

- **An iOS-style look.** Flat white and grey keys instead of the glassy ones, a thin line under each key, a hollow shift arrow, a lowercase "return" and a blue key for Go, Search, Send, Next and Done. When you press a letter the key hides under a balloon-shaped preview, like iOS.
- **Emoji.** A new emoji key opens a panel with about 600 emoji in nine categories. Tabs run along the bottom beside ABC and delete, and a Recents tab appears once you have used some (kept in memory for the session only; nothing is saved).
- **Paste.** A paste button appears next to the suggestions when the clipboard holds text. The keyboard reads the clipboard only when you tap it.
- **Symbol variants.** Hold a symbol or digit as you would a letter: a dash gives en and em dashes, $ gives other currencies, a quote gives curly and foreign quotes, 0 gives a degree sign.
- **One-handed mode.** Customize, Control panel & extras: Off, Left or Right pushes the keys to one side, a little narrower, with a button on the free side to bring the full width back.
- **Faster delete.** Hold backspace and it starts taking whole words, as on iOS. It now removes an emoji or a flag as one character.

## Overlaps and layout

- The keyboard-switcher (globe) key now appears only when Android has another keyboard to switch to, so it is not a dead key next to Android's own switcher button.
- The keys sit higher above Android's own switcher button and the gesture bar.
- The preview over the top row of keys is no longer cut off at the top of the keyboard.
- In landscape the keys are shorter and the app stays visible above the keyboard, and on an unfolded screen the keyboard stays thumb-sized instead of stretching.

## Speed

Pressing a key now redraws that key and its preview instead of the whole keyboard, deletes without asking the app whether text is selected, and uses the correction the suggestion thread already worked out instead of searching the dictionary on the main thread. The word lists load in the background so they do not slow the first draw.

## Two fixes

- Holding a key could make Android think the keyboard was covering the whole screen and hide it.
- Holding a letter with accents built a separate window in the middle of the touch, which on a slow device could get the keyboard shut down as not responding. Both now draw inside the keyboard itself.

## Notes: what this has and hasn't been tested on

On an emulator with a centred camera, in a web page's text fields: the layout in light, dark, one-handed and landscape; the preview balloon; the alternatives strip for a letter; the numbers page; the emoji panel, typing two emoji and the Recents tab; and Paste. The emulator is very slow, so how typing feels and how fast it is can only be judged on a phone. The system's own switcher button still appears under the keys on Android versions that show it; the keyboard now keeps clear of it but cannot hide it.

Not tested on a real device, so please check:

- **Typing speed and feel.** Fast typing, key hide-and-balloon on every press, and the haptics.
- **Long-press variants** on symbols (hold the dash, the dollar sign and a quote), and the alternates strip for letters.
- **The emoji panel**: scrolling, categories, and that the emoji look right in the apps you use.
- **Paste** with something copied, including that nothing pastes by itself.
- **One-handed mode** on the left and the right, and the button on the free side.
- **Return key labels** in a search box, a messaging app and a form (Go, Search, Send, Next, Done).
- **An unfolded screen** if you have one.
