# Uno Launcher 0.20.0-uno01

Widget stacks, search from anywhere, haptics and sound design, and a more alive Dynamic Island.

## What's new

- **Widget stacks.** Long-press a widget, choose **Stack another widget here**, and pick another widget. They share one spot, with a dot rail on the right edge to switch (tap a dot or drag along it). Up to five widgets per stack.
- **Search from anywhere.** A glass search panel over any page: apps, contacts (if you turned that on), answers to sums and conversions, and headlines from your saved feeds. Open it from the island's Search button, or in Customize > Control panel & extras choose **Search** for the left-hand pull-down (notifications stay the default).
- **Search suggestions.** When no installed app matches, Spotlight and All apps offer "Search F-Droid", "Search Aurora Store" and "Search the web" (your chosen browser, Vanadium by default). Tapping one opens that app with your words. Uno doesn't show those results itself and contacts nothing, so your no-hosts rule holds; Customize > Control panel & extras > Search suggestions picks the browser and stores.
- **Haptics and sound.** Ticks on page changes, app launches, opening the panel and flipping Focus, all behind one Haptics switch. Optional sounds (off by default) use Android's own touch sounds, so nothing is bundled and they follow your system settings.
- **Dynamic island.** A ring around the pill: orange and depleting for a timer, green and filling to your battery level while charging. Notification peeks show the app's icon. Tap the track name (with notification access on) to open the player. Timers that finish while the app was stopped now finish properly.

## Notes: what this release has and hasn't been tested on

Tested only on an emulator that renders in software (slow, with occasional "not responding" dialogs); real-hardware performance is unmeasured.

Verified on the emulator: search suggestions for an uninstalled app (the Google Play and Chrome rows appeared with the right names, and the web row launched Chrome's search); stacking two real provider widgets (the stack saved, the rail appeared, tapping a dot switched pages, and both widget ids stayed bound across a restart); Spotlight from the left pull-down ranking Maps above Gmail for "ma"; the island's charging and timer rings (starting at the top centre, clockwise); the new settings page; and the ranking, stack, ring and feedback rules by unit test.

Please check on your Pixel:

- **The F-Droid and Aurora rows.** Neither app exists on the emulator, so the rows' `market://search` hand-off is untested against them (the emulator's stub Play Store ignored it). If tapping a store row says it can't open searches, or opens the wrong screen, tell me which store.
- **Vanadium's web search.** I tested Chrome; if Vanadium doesn't take Android's web-search request, Uno falls back to whichever app does, and I want to know.

- **Stacks with your own widgets**, especially after restarting the launcher and after removing the base widget (its stack goes with it). A layout export does not include stacks yet.
- **Haptics and sounds**: do the ticks feel right, is anything too much? I could not feel or hear anything on an emulator.
- **The island ring** at your cutout, and **tapping the track** (needs notification access on) to open your player.
- **Spotlight's feed and contact results** and the keyboard behaviour on your phone.
- Everything still unverified from earlier releases: split screen with your apps, flashlight, track titles, real badges and notification peeks, the tilt highlight, and the control panel dimming at both edges.
