# Uno Launcher 0.23.4-uno01

The island over other apps can be tapped, no longer covers your status icons, and its controls work.

## What was wrong

- **The overlay island could not be tapped, and sat under the status icons.** Android draws a normal overlay beneath the status bar, and the status bar owns every touch in its top strip. The island sits on the camera, inside that strip, so the status icons were drawn over it and taps never reached it.
- **Pause did nothing.** The play/pause key was sent without a timestamp, which Android can treat as stale and drop.
- **Tapping an app icon on the island did nothing**, and tapping elsewhere did not close an expanded island.

## What's fixed

- Over other apps the island is now drawn by the **Uno Launcher shade gestures** accessibility service, as an accessibility overlay, which Android layers above the status bar. If you already enabled that service for the pull-down gestures, the island is tappable with no new permission. It draws only the island and reads nothing from other apps. Without the service the old overlay still works, but it cannot be tapped over the camera; the "Island everywhere" dialog has a **Tappable island** button that opens Accessibility settings.
- Tapping anywhere outside an expanded island collapses it, on Home and over other apps.
- The collapsed island no longer shows the time. The expanded panel still does.
- Over other apps the idle island shrinks to a ring around the camera so it does not cover the icons on either side. It widens for an event, timer, stopwatch, alarm, call or playback.
- Play, pause, previous and next use the playing app's own controls when notification access is on, falling back to the media key with a proper timestamp.
- Tapping the app icon in a notification peek opens that app.

## Notes: what this has and hasn't been tested on

Verified on an emulator: the island window sits above the system status bar window in the input stack, tapping it over Chrome expands it, and tapping elsewhere collapses it within a second. The geometry for the compact idle width is unit-tested.

Not tested on your phone, so please check:

- **That the island is tappable over other apps** with the accessibility service enabled (turn the service off and on once after installing if it was already on).
- **That pause, play and skip work**, with and without notification access.
- **That tapping a peek's app icon opens the app.** Android can refuse to open an app from the background, in which case the tap does nothing.
- **The idle ring** clears your status icons on both sides, and the widened island looks right when something goes live.
- **Collapse on outside tap** on Home.
