# Uno Launcher 0.23.5-uno01

Fixes a crash on launch in 0.23.4.

## What was wrong

0.23.4 made the idle island over other apps shrink to a ring around the camera. On some phones that ring is narrower than the clearance the island keeps around the camera hole, which made a position clamp invalid, and the app crashed a few seconds after it started ("Cannot coerce value to an empty range").

## What's fixed

- The idle island's width always includes the clearance around the camera, and the clamp can no longer fail on a narrow island.
- A new test sweeps screen densities, camera sizes and positions, size settings and widths, and fails on the 0.23.4 code. All 31 geometry tests pass.

Everything else from 0.23.4 is unchanged: the island over other apps is tappable through the accessibility service, tapping outside collapses it, the collapsed island no longer shows the time, and the media controls and peek app icon work.
