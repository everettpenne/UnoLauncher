# Uno Launcher 0.99.0-uno01

An iOS Home layout with iPhone-style customization, clear glass, and an island that opens out of itself and closes back in.

## What's changed

- **Home layout: Side dock or iOS** (Launcher settings › Home). The iOS layout is a strict iPhone grid scaled from the iPhone's measurements, widgets only at **Small (2×2), Medium (4×2) and Large (4×4)**, and the dock as a clear glass bar along the bottom with a **Search** pill above it that turns into the page dots while you swipe. The status bar shows instead of the status rail. The inner screen of a foldable keeps the side dock. Widgets you placed before keep their size until you resize them; a widget that fits none of the iOS sizes keeps its own.
- **Customizing Home like an iPhone** (iOS layout): hold empty space to enter jiggle mode; widgets wiggle and can be removed with their **−**; **Edit** (top left) adds a widget or apps and opens Customize; **Done** (top right) finishes. Tap a widget to switch it between Small, Medium and Large.
- **Clear glass** (Appearance, on by default): Home's glass is clear with a bright edge. Sheets and panels keep their tint.
- **The island and search open out of the island and close back into it**, from wherever and however big it is, anchored to the camera. Search keeps the island's shape as it grows. When a sheet or panel takes over Home, the island closes and fades instead of vanishing.
- **The open island**: a battery gauge, proper buttons, a soft shadow, and a long press on the pill opens the app behind what it shows.
- Fixed: after a peek, the island's touch area could stay bigger than the island and take taps meant for the app underneath.

## Notes: what this has and hasn't been tested on

Unit tests (984) and lint pass. Checked on an Android 17 emulator with a punch-hole cutout: the island's open and close (slowed six times), search opening from the open island, the iOS layout, its dock, Search pill and page dots, jiggle mode, the Edit menu and widget sizes. **Not run on a real phone.** On the emulator, with the status bar showing in the iOS layout, the island covered the status bar's clock; the island's edges are tuned to the Pixel 10a's status bar, so check it there.
