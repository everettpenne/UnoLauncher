# Uno Launcher 0.24.0-uno01

Live Updates in the island, a full-screen rule, backup of your settings, tinted icons and richer folders.

## What's new

- **Live Updates (Android 16+).** A ride, delivery, timer or navigation that an app posts as a Live Update shows in the island as its icon and short status, and opens into a card with progress. Needs notification access; there is a switch.
- **Full screen.** The island steps aside in landscape (full-screen video, most games). Full-screen apps in portrait cannot be detected without reading other apps' windows, which Uno does not do.
- **Backup includes your settings.** Save and Restore now carry the island, keyboard, panel and appearance choices. Location, browser choice and debug options are not included, and widget stacks are not (their widgets belong to this phone).
- **Tinted icons.** Icons take their colour from your wallpaper, independent of Color from wallpaper.
- **Folders and stacks.** 3x3 folder previews with one combined badge; stacks fade between widgets and show the name.
- **Developer HUD** in debug builds only.

## Download and install

Use **UnoLauncher-0.24.0-uno01-release.apk** from the release assets. Signed with the same key as previous Uno releases, so it installs directly over 0.23.10. It still cannot install over a jakesgoodapps Duo Launcher build: uninstall that build first.

## Notes: what this has and hasn't been tested on

Verified on an emulator: Live Update glyph and card, landscape hiding and return, the HUD. Unit tests cover the new logic. Not tested on a real device: how Live Updates from real apps look, whether your games and videos switch to landscape, restoring a backup across two phones, and the feel of the stack fade.
