# Uno Launcher 0.16.13-uno01

Glass text that adapts to the wallpaper behind it, and a soft blur toward the screen edges.

## What's new

- **Adaptive glass text.** Widgets, the status rail, the page-dots strip and the search button now switch between white and dark ink depending on how bright the wallpaper is behind them, so they stay readable on pale wallpapers instead of washing out. It uses contrast, not taste: dark ink takes over where it reads better than white, and it fades rather than snapping, with a small dead band so it never flickers while you swipe.
- **Progressive edge blur.** Content now softens into a blur toward the top and bottom edges of the screen, under the island, rail, dock and page dots, like iOS 26's scroll edge effect. It's subtle at rest and shows when something scrolls under an edge.

## Notes

- The glass selection lens (a lens that follows your finger along the page dots and dock) is not in this release.
- The edge blur is drawn by the GPU every frame; I haven't measured its battery or frame-time cost on a physical device. If you see any slowdown, turn off **Liquid glass**, which turns it off too.
- This release is validated on emulators; treat it as a beta and report problems with your device model and Android version.

## Privacy

Uno has no account service, analytics, or automatic crash upload. See the [privacy notes](../PRIVACY.md).
