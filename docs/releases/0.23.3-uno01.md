# Uno Launcher 0.23.3-uno01

The everywhere-island sits on the camera at the top, and stays on it when you turn the phone.

## What was wrong

- **Too low in other apps.** The overlay window was laid out in the area below the status bar, so the island's position, which is measured from the very top of the screen, was applied from the bottom of the status bar instead. That left it about a status bar's height below the camera, as in your browser screenshot.
- **Hopping when you turn the phone.** Turned sideways, the camera is on the left or right edge. The code only recognised a camera near the top of the screen, so it found none and put a horizontal island at the top-centre of the rotated screen, nowhere near the camera.

## What's fixed

- The overlay window now uses absolute screen positions (`FLAG_LAYOUT_IN_SCREEN` and `FLAG_LAYOUT_NO_LIMITS`), so the island lines up with the camera. It is still not focusable and still passes touches outside its own bounds.
- Turn the phone sideways and the island stays with the camera: a vertical pill on the left or right edge, centred on it. Events and the expanded panel open inward from that edge, with the time, date and battery beside the camera. This applies on Home as well as over other apps, and the overlay re-reads the display each time, so rotating either way updates it.

- **The island size setting works again.** The overlay used the camera's raw bounding rectangle, which is often tall and starts at the screen's top edge, so there was no room for the slider to change anything. It now tightens to the actual hole like Home does.

## Notes: what this has and hasn't been tested on

Verified on an emulator, with a centred notch: over Chrome the island's top is at the top of the screen and it is centred on the notch (it spans x=251 to 829 around a notch at x=414 to 666); turned to landscape one way it is a vertical pill on the left edge centred on the notch, turned the other way it is on the right edge, and tapping it opens the panel inward within the screen. The geometry rules, including that a waterfall strip is not mistaken for a camera, are unit-tested.

The size-setting fix is verified by a unit test only (the emulator's notch touches the top edge, so it cannot show the slider's effect); please try the slider on your phone.

Not tested on your phone, so please check:

- **That the island lines up with your real camera in portrait**, especially its height. The emulator's notch is not a punch hole.
- **Landscape**: does the vertical pill sit on the camera, in both rotations? The inward panel hides the action buttons (it is the overlay panel), as in portrait.
- **Fold or unfold**: the overlay re-reads the display, but I did not test a fold.
- A camera in a corner, which is treated as a top cutout.

This is a beta; the geometry is the part I would expect to need a nudge on real hardware.
