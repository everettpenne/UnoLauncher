# Uno Launcher 0.142.0-uno01

App colours back on Hyper Glass logos, a Circle icon shape, a glass edge that bends on My feed and the App Library, and a Hyper Glass style for widgets.

## What's changed

### Hyper Glass icons: the app's colours come back

Hyper Glass takes an icon's plate away and keeps its logo. On many icons the colour was on the plate and the logo itself was white (Settings' gear on blue; Spice's eye on a blue-to-purple gradient), so in **App Colors on Black** and **App Colors on Clear** those logos came out white.

- A white or grey logo now takes the colour of the plate that was under it, at each spot, so a gradient plate gives a gradient logo. The colour is brightened enough to read on black or on clear glass.
- Parts of a logo with colour of their own keep it (Spice's orange wave, Chrome, Gmail, YouTube), and a logo on a white or grey plate is left as it is.

### Circle icons

**Appearance › App Icons › Icon Shape** has **Circle**, with every other style as well as Hyper Glass. Mixed is unchanged: each app keeps the shape it had.

### Hyper Glass Cards (experimental): the edge bends

With **Hyper Glass Cards** on, the edge of My feed and of the App Library bends what is under it (the wallpaper and the grain) in towards the edge, as the Hyper Glass icons' glass does, with the same faint blue light inside the rim. The words and buttons on the card are not bent. The wallpaper under a card is blurred, so the bend shows mostly in the grain.

### Hyper Glass widgets (experimental)

A widget style beside Card and Glass: the grain, the bending edge and the split of colour of the cards. Choose it for one widget (long-press it, **Edit Widget**, **Style**), or for all of them under **Appearance › Widgets** (there it is labelled **Hyper**).

- For the first few seconds after the launcher starts it is drawn as Glass, until the picture of the wallpaper it uses is ready.
- A widget in one of Material's shapes stays Card or Glass.
- It does not drift, so Home at rest draws nothing for it.
- Android 13 or later; below that it is Glass.

The widget options' Style and Shape choices now wrap onto a second line instead of running off the side of the sheet, where the last of them could not be seen.

### Fixes

- **My feed's corners.** A faint sheen across the top of the card showed as a pale square past each rounded top corner. It is clipped to the card now.

## Privacy

Nothing new is read, kept or sent.

## Notes: what this has and hasn't been tested on

Everything here was tested on an emulator (Android 16) with the test build (Uno Dev). **None of it has been run on a phone.**

**Icon colours** were checked by rendering twelve apps' icons before and after, and on Home in the rounded square and in Circle.

**Hyper Glass widgets** were seen on Home's clock, date and battery widgets, in dark and light. Not checked: widgets with buttons (Media, Timers, Counter), widgets from other apps, and what they cost while pages swipe. In light mode the coloured captions on them (the orange "Local time", the red day name) are a little faint.

**The bending edge** was looked at closely on My feed; the App Library uses the same drawing.

Once, while testing, the launcher stopped responding with a widget's options open. It happened while a screen-reading test tool was running on a heavily loaded computer, and did not happen again after a restart, but its cause was not confirmed.

1,546 unit tests pass and lint is clean.
