# Uno Launcher 0.23.8-uno01

Fixes the island opening from the wrong corner and collapsing through the wrong place over other apps.

## What was wrong

When Android resizes an overlay window, it shows the window's old, smaller picture anchored at the new window's top-left corner until the new picture is ready. 0.23.7 resized the island's window as the island opened and closed, so for a moment the island appeared in the wrong place: it opened from a corner, and on closing it shrank toward the right, jumped to the left, and then slid back to the camera.

## What's fixed

- Over other apps, with the camera on top, the island is drawn in a window of one fixed size that never resizes while the island moves. That window takes no touches, so it does not block the app underneath.
- A separate, transparent window follows the island and takes the touches (tap, long-press, swipe up, the buttons in the open island, and tapping outside to close). It draws nothing, so changing its size every frame cannot be seen.
- The overlay now starts at the top centre rather than flashing at the top-left corner.
- A side-edge camera (phone turned sideways) still uses a single window sized to the island.

## Notes: what this has and hasn't been tested on

On an emulator with a centred punch hole at the Pixel's resolution: a screen recording of the island opening and closing stays centred on the camera throughout; tap opens it, long-press opens the tools, swipe up closes it, and tapping outside collapses it. The input dispatcher shows the drawing window as not touchable and the touch window at the island's size.

The emulator is very slow, so how smooth it feels, and whether any corner clipping remains, can only be judged on a phone. Not retested through the new touch window: the media buttons and the camera and microphone marks, the flick that turns the live activities, and a notification peek's app icon.

Please check on your phone:

- **Open and close** over a browser: no corner, no jump, round corners throughout.
- **Taps around the island** still reach the app underneath, for example the browser's address bar just beside the camera.
- **The buttons in the open island**, and the flick between live activities.
