# Uno Launcher 0.131.0-uno01

Ask Qwen knows what your question needs from the phone.

## What's changed

- **Phone context for Ask Qwen**: with each question, Uno gives Qwen (in [Uno Lab](https://github.com/everettpenne/UnoLab), on the phone) the facts it points at, read at that moment. Always: date and time, battery, next alarm, Focus/Do Not Disturb/ringer. Only for a question about them: your calendar (with Calendar access), location, recent notifications, installed apps, screen time, storage, network, your note and checklist, the clipboard and more.
- Each kind has its own switch under Extras › Search › **What Qwen Can See**, all on once the assistant is on. The facts go to Uno Lab with that one question (at most about 1,000 characters) and are never stored or sent over the network.
- Messages, headlines and the clipboard are written by others, so they are marked as quotes and never go with a request Qwen may turn into an action.
- Needs Uno Lab 0.3.0. With Uno Lab 0.2.0 the question goes alone, as in 0.130.0.
- Includes everything in 0.130.0-uno01.
